# Polish and other CEE migrant workers / migrant ZZP'ers in the Netherlands: how they find work, barriers, exploitation, unmet needs (2024-2026)

> Method note (read first): research date 2026-11-28. Page fetching was blocked in this session (cbs.nl and eib.nl did not resolve; they are outside the session's allowed network domains), so every finding below rests on search-result extracts of the cited pages, not on a full read of the documents. Figures marked "verify" should be checked against the primary PDF before publication. Statistics and anecdote/commercial claims are labelled separately in each section.

## 1. How many Poles and other EU labour migrants work in the Netherlands, in which sectors, and how many are ZZP (esp. construction)?

### Takeaway
Depending on definition, the Netherlands has roughly 0.7-1.0 million labour migrants (SEO/ABU: 683,404 in 2024; ABU/NBBU factsheet: 984,169 in 2022; CBS Migrantenmonitor: 1.6 million immigrant employees incl. long-stayers at end-2023), with Poles the largest group and about 42% working via temp agencies. No official count of Polish ZZP'ers exists, because CBS does not classify entrepreneurs by nationality; the only number found is an older CBS figure of about 15.7 thousand Polish self-employed.

### Cited Findings
STATISTICS
- CBS Migrantenmonitor: about 1.6 million immigrants worked as employees in NL at end-2023 (includes people not registered with a municipality); about 720 thousand when immigrants resident longer than five years are excluded — [CBS, Opvattingen over arbeidsmigranten, ch. 2 (2026)](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/diversen/2026/opvattingen-over-arbeidsmigranten/2-hoeveel-arbeidsmigranten-telt-nederland-)
- Migrantenmonitor editions exist for 2023, 2024 (reference years 2007-2024) and 2025; the Poland-specific tables are in these custom datasets (not readable in this session) — [CBS Migrantenmonitor 2024](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/maatwerk/2025/43/migrantenmonitor-2024); [CBS Migrantenmonitor 2025](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/maatwerk/2026/44/migrantenmonitor-2025)
- ABU/NBBU factsheet (CBS microdata processed by SEO): 683,404 labour migrants in NL in 2024, of which 546,535 EU/EFTA (per document title; verify split); 55% work in regular employment, 42% as temp agency workers — [ABU factsheet "Arbeidsmigranten in NL" (Nov 2026)](https://www.abu.nl/app/uploads/2026/11/Arbeidsmigranten-in-NL-factsheet.pdf)
- SEO (2026): more than 315 thousand labour migrants worked as agency workers in NL in 2024, mainly from Poland, Romania and Bulgaria — [SEO, Arbeidsmigranten in de uitzendsector (2026-41)](https://www.seo.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-41-Arbeidsmigranten-in-de-uitzendsector.-Onderzoek-naar-omvang-en-kenmerken.pdf)
- Earlier ABU/NBBU factsheet: 984,169 labour migrants in 2022 vs 735,529 in 2020; ABU/NBBU members place about 34% of all labour migrants and 72% of agency-placed labour migrants (different definition from the 2024 figure, not comparable) — [ABU/NBBU factsheet 2023](https://www.artra.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Arbeidsmigranten-in-Nederland-factsheet-ABU-NBBU-2023.pdf)
- CBS (2026): in 2024 there were over 2.3 thousand temp agencies accounting for 407 thousand agency jobs; more than half of agency work was done by foreign-born agency workers living in NL under eight years, mostly from Poland, Romania, Ukraine or Bulgaria; at a quarter of agencies more than half of agency workers are not registered as Dutch residents — [CBS news 2026/05](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/05/in-buitenland-geboren-uitzendkrachten-doen-ruim-helft-uitzendwerk)
- SZW annual report on labour migrants (Nov 2024): about half of the agency-employed migrants in the Migrantenmonitor (reference Dec 2023) come from Poland (101 thousand); 96,290 posted workers were active in NL in 2023, with posting companies mainly established in Poland and Germany — [SZW Jaarrapportage arbeidsmigranten 2024](https://dossierarbeidsmigranten.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/SZW-IPA-jaarrapportage-2024.pdf)
- Population: on 1 Jan 2024, 745.5 thousand people with another EU nationality lived in NL; Poles were the largest group at 194.4 thousand (85.8 thousand in 2014) — [CBS news 2024/23](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2024/23/ruim-745-duizend-mensen-met-andere-eu-nationaliteit-in-nederland)
- Older CBS study for SZW: 261.5 thousand Poles, of whom 213.6 thousand had a job, including 15.7 thousand self-employed (zzp). Reference year not visible in the extract; the CBS "Poolse werknemers in Nederland" publication dates from 2018 — [CBS, Poolse werknemers in Nederland (2018)](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/achtergrond/2018/18/poolse-werknemers-in-nederland)
- The number of Polish ZZP'ers is "not exactly known because CBS does not classify entrepreneurs by nationality" — [MT/Sprout on eFaktura](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/efaktura-zzp-boekhouden-polen)
- Total ZZP market: 1,825,347 zzp'ers in the KVK register on 30 Sep 2026 (69% of all registered businesses; 82% of new registrations in Q3 2026) — [KVK press release](https://www.kvk.nl/en/press/82-per-cent-of-starters-is-zzper-despite-tackling-of-false-self-employment/); [KVK ZZP Monitor](https://www.kvk.nl/en/figures-and-trends/kvk-zzp-monitor/)
- Construction: about 158,000 self-employed out of about 530 thousand people working in construction in Q1 2026 — [Monitor Bouwketen voorjaar 2026](https://www.technieknederland.nl/media/pe2gdgfr/monitor-bouwketen-voorjaar-2026-definitief.pdf); EIB projects 157,000 self-employed labour-years in 2025, 158,000 in 2026, 165,000 in 2029 and notes resumed enforcement may make NL less attractive to foreign zzp'ers — [EIB Trends op de bouwarbeidsmarkt 2025-2029](https://www.eib.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/EIB-rapport-Trends-op-de-bouwarbeidsmarkt-2025-2029.pdf)
- Sectors: labour migrants in Zuid-Holland work mostly in logistics, construction and production — [SEO 2026-31](https://www.seo.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-31-Woon-en-werkplaats-arbeidsmigranten-in-ZH.pdf); UWV (older) lists temp sector, agriculture and transport as the main sectors for Poles — [UWV](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/publicaties/kennis/2015/dienstverbanden-en-ww-uitkeringen-onder-europese-werknemers)
- Annual inflow of labour migrants rose from about 20,000 in 2006 to almost 70,000 in 2022 — [Eerste Kamer document](https://eerstekamer.nl/9370000/1/j4nvgs5kjg27kof_j9vvkfvj6b325az/vmp6ogs9m6xv)

### Inferences
- With about 15.7k Polish self-employed in the older CBS count and the Polish population more than doubling since 2014, the current number of Polish ZZP'ers is plausibly in the tens of thousands, but this is an extrapolation, not a measured figure.
- Polish ZZP'ers are a minority of Polish workers in NL: the dominant route is agency employment (about 100k Poles in agency jobs vs. a low five-figure ZZP count).
- Foreign self-employed working temporarily in NL without KVK registration (posted, notified via postedworkers.nl) are an additional, poorly measured pool.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 count of Polish (or Romanian/Bulgarian/Ukrainian) ZZP'ers, in total or in construction (SBI F). KVK does not publish registrations by nationality in the sources found.
- Poland-specific employee totals for 2024/2025 from the Migrantenmonitor could not be read (cbs.nl blocked). A figure of 240,660 employees at Dec 2023 appeared in an Eerste Kamer document but the extract did not confirm it refers to Poles — unverified.
- Sector split per nationality (construction, installation, cleaning, logistics) not found.

## 2. How do they find work (agencies, Polish intermediaries, Facebook/WhatsApp groups, portals, word of mouth) and how big are these channels?

### Takeaway
The measurable channel is the temp agency: about 42% of labour migrants and roughly 100 thousand Poles work through one, in a market of 2.3 thousand (CBS) to about 14 thousand (Polish press) agencies. ZZP offers aimed at Poles circulate on Polish job boards (Pracuj.pl, OLX, zarabiaj.pl, Jooble) and Marktplaats, often bundled as "ZZP / A1 / umowa o pracę" with housing; no reliable data on Facebook/WhatsApp group sizes was found.

### Cited Findings
STATISTICS
- 42% of labour migrants worked as agency workers in 2024 — [ABU factsheet](https://www.abu.nl/app/uploads/2026/11/Arbeidsmigranten-in-NL-factsheet.pdf)
- CBS counts over 2.3 thousand temp agencies / 407 thousand agency jobs in 2024 — [CBS 2026/05](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/05/in-buitenland-geboren-uitzendkrachten-doen-ruim-helft-uitzendwerk); Polish press cites about 14 thousand employment agencies in NL, most without any certification — [Money.pl](https://www.money.pl/gospodarka/niderlandy-tyrali-po-13-godzin-dziennie-stracili-prace-nie-moga-doprosic-sie-o-swoje-pieniadze-6633223218702976a.html). The two numbers use different definitions (CBS agency-sector firms vs all labour lenders).

ANECDOTE / LISTING EVIDENCE
- Polish job boards carry many NL construction offers: Pracuj.pl (roofers/carpenters "from 25 EUR/hour"; also B2B contracts), OLX Praca Holandia section including B2B/self-employment offers, Jooble aggregations — [Pracuj.pl listing](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/dekarz-ciesla-dachowy-ciesla-budowalny-holandia,oferta,12156128); [OLX Praca Holandia](https://www.olx.pl/praca/holandia/); [Jooble](https://pl.jooble.org/praca-stan-bud/Holandia)
- Typical intermediary ad on a free Polish board: construction workers (drywall fitters, roofers) offered "ZZP / A1 / umowa o pracę", 650-850 EUR per week, with accommodation and weekly payouts — [zarabiaj.pl listing](https://www.zarabiaj.pl/oferta/oferujemy-wspolprace-na-zasadach-zzp-a1-umowa-o-prace-6a54e03eb444b/); [zarabiaj.pl listing 2](https://www.zarabiaj.pl/oferta/obecnie-poszukuje-konkretnych-sprawdzonych-pracownikow-budowlanych-6a2945685b410/)
- Polish-language ZZP assignment posted on Marktplaats ("Stolarz nowe budownictwo ZZP", 38-44 EUR/hour) — [Marktplaats](https://www.marktplaats.nl/v/vacatures/vacatures-bouwnijverheid/m2423548261-stolarz-nowe-budownictwo-zzp)
- Recruitment inside Poland by third-party recruiters, with pay and duties misrepresented until shortly before departure (case reported to NOS) — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2432771)
- Jooble has dedicated result pages for "praca Facebook Holandia" / "praca grupa Holandia", indicating job seekers search for Facebook-group-style offers — [Jooble](https://pl.jooble.org/praca-facebook/Holandia)

### Inferences
- A Pole who wants ZZP assignments has no dedicated, trusted channel: offers are mixed into employee job boards and classifieds and are mostly posted by intermediaries rather than end clients.
- The "ZZP / A1 / contract" bundling shows the legal form is chosen by the intermediary, which is the pattern Dutch regulators treat as a bogus self-employment risk.

### Gaps
- Member counts of Facebook groups ("Praca w Holandii", "Polacy w Holandii", "ZZP Holandia") and traffic of niedziela.nl / Polonia.nl: no reliable source found. These must be read directly from the platforms.
- No survey quantifying the share of migrants finding work via word of mouth, WhatsApp or social media vs agencies. The Risbo survey may contain this but could not be read.
- Named agencies (OTTO Work Force, Covebo, E&A, Tempo-Team, Carrière): no 2024-2026 placement volumes found.

## 3. Barriers (language, network, certificates, registration, housing, banking, tax, insurance, trust, discrimination)

### Takeaway
Language is the best-documented barrier: more than half of labour migrants know little or no Dutch. For ZZP'ers the hard structural barriers are regulatory (DBA enforcement since 2025, a planned hourly-rate presumption around 36-38 EUR, posting notification) rather than documented discrimination, for which no data was found.

### Cited Findings
STATISTICS
- Kenniscentrum Arbeidsmigranten poll of 1,550 labour migrants (2023): more than half know no or little Dutch; in 66% of cases contact with the municipality is in English — [Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/sociaal/inclusiviteit/arbeidsmigrant-spreekt-engels-met-de-gemeente)
- Language is named as the main barrier to both finding information and accessing care, and migrants often lack the right channels to find information independently — [De Haagse Hogeschool, Mobiel Informatiepunt 2023-2024](https://www.dehaagsehogeschool.nl/media/mobiel-informatiepunt-2023-2024)
- At a quarter of temp agencies more than half of agency workers are not registered as residents (no BRP registration) — [CBS 2026/05](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/05/in-buitenland-geboren-uitzendkrachten-doen-ruim-helft-uitzendwerk)

REGULATION
- Since 1 Jan 2025 the Belastingdienst again checks whether zzp'ers are genuinely self-employed (enforcement moratorium ended) — [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/positie-van-zzper-wordt-duidelijker/)
- Legal presumption of employment based on hourly rate: KVK states that below 38 EUR/hour (reference date 1 Jan 2026) the client must prove there is no employment contract if the zzp'er invokes the presumption — [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/ben-je-wel-zelfstandig-met-een-uurtarief-lager-dan-38-euro/); NOS reported the cabinet threshold as 36 EUR — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2574022). Sources conflict on the amount (36 vs 38 EUR) and on the in-force date; a commercial source says the Senate adopted the act on 16 June 2026 — [Access Financial](https://accessfinancial.com/pl/slowniczek/wet-dba-holandia/). A low rate is not prohibited in itself.
- Foreign self-employed working temporarily in NL must notify the assignment on postedworkers.nl before starting; the portal is in Dutch, English or German (not Polish), and construction is a sector with a notification duty — [WagwEU factsheet for ZZP'ers, Polish version](https://www.postedworkers.nl/binaries/postedworkers/documenten/publicaties/2023/08/09/factsheet-buitenlandse-zzper-pools/WagWEU_Factsheet+ZZP%27ers_Pools_2024.pdf)
- Clients' reluctance: companies increasingly hesitate to hire zzp'ers because of DBA enforcement — [Accountant.nl (2024)](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2024/9/bedrijven-aarzelen-al-vaker-bij-inhuren-zzper/); construction deregistrations are linked to announced stricter enforcement — [Monitor Bouwketen voorjaar 2026](https://www.technieknederland.nl/media/pe2gdgfr/monitor-bouwketen-voorjaar-2026-definitief.pdf)
- Bouwplaats-ID: the ministry concluded on 30 Oct 2025 that a statutory site ID is "too untargeted" and not proportionate; a CAO-based obligation would not cover painters, kitchen installers or zzp'ers; FNV, CNV and AFNL asked for reconsideration on 28 Nov 2025, arguing only people with the right papers and certificates would then enter sites — [FNV/CNV/AFNL letter](https://www.fnv.nl/getmedia/a1db2e0a-76f8-42ad-8de8-d74a90474c37/25-11-28-brief-FNV-CNV-AFNL-aan-SZW-oproep-heroverweging-besluit-Bouwplaats-ID.pdf); [Kamerbrief via FNV](https://www.fnv.nl/getmedia/baf81dc1-2545-4620-89b9-1205bb3ec9e7/WAR249-Kamerbrief-stand-van-zaken-Bouwplaats-ID-en-inhouding-huisvesting.pdf)
- Housing dependency: the minister reversed the predecessor's decision to end the deduction of housing costs from the minimum wage, keeping the deduction for now; this was strongly criticised in the Senate — [Eerste Kamer debate 11 Nov 2025](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/verslagdeel/20251111/wet_toelating_2)

### Inferences
- A Polish ZZP'er charging under about 36-38 EUR/hour becomes a legal risk for the Dutch client, so low-rate Polish ZZP'ers are the group most likely to lose direct assignments or be pushed back into agency/payroll constructions.
- There is no sector-wide, portable proof of identity and certificates (no mandatory Bouwplaats-ID), so proving reliability and qualifications to a new Dutch client remains ad hoc. This is a gap a marketplace profile with verified KVK/BTW/VCA/insurance could fill.
- Official onboarding for foreign ZZP'ers is partly not available in Polish (postedworkers.nl portal), reinforcing dependence on Polish accounting offices and intermediaries.

### Gaps
- No data found on: recognition problems with VCA/SCC, NEN 3140 or gas certificates for Poles; difficulty opening a Dutch bank account; take-up of AOV/liability insurance among Polish ZZP'ers; discrimination against Polish ZZP'ers by clients; distrust of platforms.
- BSN/KVK/BTW registration friction: only general guidance pages found, no measured friction.

## 4. Exploitation and failures; Roemer follow-up; Wtta status

### Takeaway
The Wtta (agency admission system) was adopted (Tweede Kamer 15 Apr 2025, Eerste Kamer 11 Nov 2025) and enters into force 1 Jan 2027, with applications due before 1 July 2027 and enforcement most likely from 1 Jan 2028. Exploitation remains documented (285 signals of possible labour exploitation in 2024; Poles and Romanians in the top-five nationalities), and construction research flags foreign ZZP'ers placed via intermediary bureaus as a bogus self-employment risk group.

### Cited Findings
STATISTICS / OFFICIAL
- Wtta in force 1 Jan 2027; lenders must apply for admission before 1 July 2027 to use transitional law — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/04/24/vanaf-2027-strengere-regels-uitzendbureaus); [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2025/05/toelatingsstelsel-uitzendbureaus-per-1-januari-2027)
- Enforcement date conflict: Ondernemersplein and Staatsblad 2026, 159 indicate enforcement from 1 Jan 2028 — [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/uitzendbureaus-moeten-gecertificeerd-zijn/); a 2026 parliamentary letter says the Arbeidsinspectie can start enforcing from 1 Jan 2027, and an Eerste Kamer timeline mentions 1 July 2027 for inspection bodies — [Eerste Kamer dossier 36446](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36446_wet_toelating?df2=kst). In April 2026 the ministry considered but rejected further delay — [kst 36446 D](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-36446-d.html)
- Introduction was already postponed once (originally 2026) — [Kiwa](https://www.kiwa.com/nl-be/nl/over-kiwa/nieuws/invoering-toelatingsstelsel-wtta-uitgesteld-naar-2026/); on 8 Oct 2026 an internet consultation opened on changing the requirements for when companies may lend staff — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/10/08/internetconsultatie-wijziging-eisen-wanneer-bedrijven-personeel-mogen-uitlenen)
- Roemer follow-up: cabinet letter of 20 Nov 2025 states the Aanjaagteam recommendations are largely implemented or in progress; the administrative director ban was implemented differently — [Tweede Kamer document](https://www.tweedekamer.nl/downloads/document?id=2026D56288)
- Criticism: municipalities, unions and Roemer say the law does not go far enough and proper registration of labour migrants is still missing — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2559150); "since Roemer it has only got worse" — [Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/sociaal/sinds-roemer-het-alleen-maar-slechter-geworden)
- Arbeidsinspectie investigation service received 285 signals of possible labour exploitation in 2024, resulting in 102 reports — [Arbeidsinspectie annual report 2024, Eerste Kamer](https://eerstekamer.nl/overig/20250320/opsporingsdienst_van_de/document)
- Reported trafficking victims rose to 944 in 2024 (868 in 2023), mainly due to labour exploitation and minors in criminal exploitation — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2586742)
- Romanians and Poles are in the top five nationalities in reports of labour exploitation / serious disadvantage (2023), with Ukrainians, Turks and Indians — [Arbeidsinspectie factsheet 2023](https://nlarbeidsinspectie.nl/binaries/nlarbeidsinspectie/documenten/publicaties/2024/08/29/factsheet-arbeidsuitbuiting-en-ernstige-benadeling-2023/factsheet-arbeidsuitbuiting-en-ernstige-benadeling-2023.pdf)
- The Arbeidsinspectie only checks statutory minimum wage; for CAO wage claims workers are referred to the Juridisch Loket — [parliamentary document](https://app.1848.nl/static/pdf/a0/ec/a0ec34ce47a63f680a0c9271f05f45f54eb280f7.pdf)
- Workplace accidents occur disproportionately among agency workers, including labour migrants — [Accountancy Vanmorgen on the 2024 annual report](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2025/03/25/arbeidsinspectie-arbeidsongevallen-vaak-bij-uitzendkrachten-waaronder-arbeidsmigranten/)
- EIB (Mar 2026): foreign zzp'ers are a hard-to-trace group with elevated bogus self-employment risk; the intermediary bureaus that place them are identifiable, so EIB calls for targeted enforcement and clear chain liability — [EIB, Schijnzelfstandigheid in de bouw en infra](https://www.eib.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Rapport-schijnzelfstandigheid-in-de-bouw-en-infra-4.pdf)
- Older benchmark: SEOR (2013) estimated bogus self-employment at 3-5% of the self-employed in construction, with foreign zzp'ers under-represented in the sample — [Kamer answers 2018](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/ah-tk-20182019-115.pdf)
- Historic construction case: one firm set up 530 sole proprietorships for Polish horticulture workers; MPs asked whether Inspectie SZW and Belastingdienst could do nothing — [Kamervragen 2018](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kv-tk-2018Z15973.pdf)

ANECDOTE / PRESS
- Money.pl: Poles worked 13-hour days, lost their jobs and could not obtain their pay from an agency; "several hundred Poles a year" fall victim to agency abuses (press claim, source of estimate not given) — [Money.pl](https://www.money.pl/gospodarka/niderlandy-tyrali-po-13-godzin-dziennie-stracili-prace-nie-moga-doprosic-sie-o-swoje-pieniadze-6633223218702976a.html)
- Common scam: required up-front payments before departure as "confirmation" of a seat on the bus to the Netherlands; fake deposits — [Bankier.pl](https://www.bankier.pl/wiadomosc/Polacy-na-saksach-zlote-gory-lewe-kaucje-i-stracone-pieniadze-7523630.html)
- Rule: an agency may charge only actual costs (travel, medical exams, document translation), never a fee for finding the job itself (older Polish government brochure) — [Zielona Linia](https://zielonalinia.gov.pl/upload/poradniki2013/praca-w-holandii.pdf)
- Fake job offers on Facebook/OLX appear as sponsored posts from private accounts or pages impersonating known companies; typical demands are a new bank account "for recruitment", verification transfers, mediation fees or deposits (general Polish warnings, not NL-specific) — [Interia/Cyberpolicja](https://geekweek.interia.pl/bezpieczenstwo/news-dziwne-oferty-pracy-na-facebooku-i-olx-cyberpolicja-ostrzega,nId,22177889); [Android.com.pl](https://android.com.pl/artykuly/746759-oferty-pracy-facebook-oszustwo-scam/)
- Polish intermediaries advertising as bookkeepers/administration offices: at least 150 Polish intermediaries facilitated benefit fraud; two in Tilburg handled about 315 people (Nieuwsuur, 2018 — dated) — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2248811)

### Inferences
- Wtta targets labour lending (agencies, payrollers). Genuine ZZP contracting is outside its scope, so stricter agency rules from 2027-2028 may push marginal intermediaries toward ZZP constructions unless DBA enforcement and the hourly-rate presumption close that route at the same time.
- Because the inspectorate does not enforce CAO wages or unpaid ZZP invoices, a Polish ZZP'er with a non-paying client has only civil remedies in a foreign language; payment protection (escrow, verified clients) is an unserved need.

### Gaps
- Arbeidsinspectie 2025 annual report figures (underpayment, agency inspections, foreign ZZP in construction) were not retrievable; the report was sent to parliament on 26 Mar 2026 — [Eerste Kamer](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/overig/20260326/jaarverslag_2025_van_de/document).
- No FairWork 2024/2025 figures on help requests by nationality found.
- No data on frequency of unpaid invoices to Polish ZZP'ers, or NL-specific investigations of fake job ads in Polish Facebook groups (NOS/NRC/FTM 2024-2026 not found).
- EIB's quantified share of bogus self-employed and of foreign zzp'ers in construction is in the report but could not be extracted.

## 5. Rates: Polish ZZP'ers vs Dutch ZZP'ers; role of accounting offices and intermediaries

### Takeaway
No statistical source compares Polish and Dutch ZZP rates in the same trade. Listing evidence suggests Polish-language ZZP construction offers at roughly 25-44 EUR/hour or 650-850 EUR/week, which sits at or below the 36-38 EUR presumption threshold, while agency CAO wages in construction run 18.95-23.78 EUR/hour.

### Cited Findings
ANECDOTE / LISTINGS (not statistics)
- Polish-language ZZP carpenter assignment (new-build): 38-44 EUR/hour depending on experience — [Marktplaats](https://www.marktplaats.nl/v/vacatures/vacatures-bouwnijverheid/m2423548261-stolarz-nowe-budownictwo-zzp)
- Roofer/carpenter offer: from 25 EUR/hour — [Pracuj.pl](https://www.pracuj.pl/praca/dekarz-ciesla-dachowy-ciesla-budowalny-holandia,oferta,12156128)
- Intermediary offers "ZZP / A1 / contract" at 650-850 EUR per week with accommodation — [zarabiaj.pl](https://www.zarabiaj.pl/oferta/oferujemy-wspolprace-na-zasadach-zzp-a1-umowa-o-prace-6a54e03eb444b/)
- Commercial claim: market rates for skilled self-employed are 60-120 EUR/hour, and below about 33-36 EUR/hour the Belastingdienst treats a low rate as an indicator of dependence — [Access Financial glossary](https://accessfinancial.com/pl/slowniczek/zzper/) (commercial advisory site; treat as indicative)

OFFICIAL
- Agency workers in construction/infra: CAO hourly wages from 18.95 to 23.78 EUR as of 1 Jan 2026 (FNV Polish-language flyer; verify job grades) — [FNV flyer Aug 2026](https://www.fnv.nl/getmedia/5ebc4a32-c7c5-4c2e-9366-ba816a52d25e/Flyer-Uitzendwerk-in-de-Bouw-en-Infra-Pools-augustus-2026.pdf)
- Hourly-rate presumption threshold 36 EUR (NOS) / 38 EUR at 1 Jan 2026 price level (KVK) — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2574022); [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/ben-je-wel-zelfstandig-met-een-uurtarief-lager-dan-38-euro/)
- Reclassification cost: a tax-advisory firm states that in construction a reclassified ZZP fee is treated as net wage, typically leading to an additional assessment of about 35% of the gross annual amount (commercial source) — [Jongbloed Fiscaal Juristen](https://www.jongbloed-fiscaaljuristen.nl/uitgelicht/schijnconstructies/fiscus_controleert_bouwsector_op_schijnzelfstandigheid/)
- Penalties for clients using non-compliant labour providers are described as about 8 thousand EUR per worker, rising above 90 thousand EUR (commercial source, unverified) — [Access Financial WAADI](https://accessfinancial.com/pl/waadi-holandia/)

### Inferences
- 650-850 EUR/week at 40-50 hours equals roughly 13-21 EUR/hour: such "ZZP" offers pay employee-level money while shifting tax, insurance and risk to the worker, and are far below the presumption threshold. The gap to the 38-44 EUR direct rate indicates what an intermediary layer can absorb.
- A marketplace giving Polish ZZP'ers direct access to end clients plus rate transparency per trade would address the largest visible income leak.

### Gaps
- No survey or dataset of actual rates earned by Polish ZZP'ers vs Dutch ZZP'ers per trade; EIB's hourly-rate chapter could not be read.
- No data on fees/commissions of Polish "biuro księgowe" offices or the margin taken by intermediaries.

## 7. What migrant workers themselves say they need

### Takeaway
The first large perception survey (Risbo/EUR, Nov 2024) finds a majority satisfied but structurally vulnerable and lagging the Dutch average, especially on mental health. Stated needs across sources centre on understandable information in their own language, independence from the employer for housing, and enforceable pay.

### Cited Findings
- Risbo/Erasmus "Zicht op arbeidsmigranten" baseline (Nov 2024): majority satisfied but vulnerable; position lags well behind the Dutch average, particularly mental health; covers EU migrants, third-country nationals and displaced Ukrainians; repeated every two years 2024-2028 (follow-up measurement 2026) — [EUR/Risbo](https://www.eur.nl/essb/nieuws/tweederangs-burgers-risbo-presenteert-resultaten-van-eerste-survey-onder-arbeidsmigranten)
- Limitation acknowledged by ministry: likely less representative for migrants in much better or much worse (possibly illegal) positions; reaching those experiencing abuses was not the assignment — [Kamerbrief 14 Nov 2024](https://flexwonen.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Kamerbrief-rapport-onderzoek-arbeidsmigranten-14-november-2024.pdf)
- Language and lack of the right channels are the main obstacles to finding information — [De Haagse Hogeschool](https://www.dehaagsehogeschool.nl/media/mobiel-informatiepunt-2023-2024); over half know little or no Dutch (n=1,550) — [Binnenlands Bestuur](https://www.binnenlandsbestuur.nl/sociaal/inclusiviteit/arbeidsmigrant-spreekt-engels-met-de-gemeente)
- Roemer's core recommendation: make labour migrants less dependent on their employer (separate work and housing) — [NOS](https://nos.nl/artikel/2354442-roemer-maak-arbeidsmigranten-minder-afhankelijk-van-hun-werkgever)
- Housing wishes surveyed among 65 labour migrants in Zuid-Holland (2020-2021, small sample) — [Provincie Zuid-Holland](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/pages/27462/8_-_wensen_en_behoeften_arbeidsmigranten_huisvesting_zh_rapportage.pdf)
- Unions want only workers with the right papers and certificates on site (Bouwplaats-ID), framing verified identity as a safety and fair-work need — [FNV/CNV/AFNL letter](https://www.fnv.nl/getmedia/a1db2e0a-76f8-42ad-8de8-d74a90474c37/25-11-28-brief-FNV-CNV-AFNL-aan-SZW-oproep-heroverweging-besluit-Bouwplaats-ID.pdf)

### Inferences
- Product-relevant unmet needs, derived from sections 2-6: Polish-language interface and contract/rights explanations; direct access to verified end clients; rate benchmarks per trade and warning below the presumption threshold; verified profile (KVK, BTW, VCA, insurance, references) as a substitute for a missing Dutch network; payment security; DBA-compliant assignment templates; independence from housing-tied intermediaries.
- These are inferences from structural evidence; no survey asked Polish ZZP'ers what they want from a marketplace.

### Gaps
- No survey of Polish ZZP'ers' own stated needs; no usable Polish forum/Facebook discussion evidence was retrieved.
- Risbo's detailed findings (recruitment channel, employer dependence, housing) and the 2026 follow-up were not readable.
- No Barka, IDHEM or CNV material found in this pass.
