# Dutch labour market structure and demand 2025-2026 (jobs, shortages, ZZP segment)

> METHOD NOTE (read first). Research date: 28 Nov 2026. In this session the hosts www.cbs.nl, www.uwv.nl and bieb.knab.nl could NOT be opened directly (network allowlist), so every figure below was taken from search-engine result extracts of those pages, not from a full read of the page. ## 1. What are the largest occupations and sectors by employment in the Netherlands (CBS data)?

### Takeaway
By occupation class, the Dutch workforce is dominated by business/administrative, care & welfare and technical occupations (each above 1.1 million employees in 2025); commercial, service and transport/logistics classes follow. Detailed counts for individual occupations (e.g. shop assistants, warehouse loaders) were not retrievable in this session.

### Cited Findings
- Employees by occupation class, 2025 (CBS table 86355NED "Werknemers; uurloon en beroep", BRC 2014 ed. 2025; employees only, excludes self-employed): Bedrijfseconomische en administratieve beroepen ~1,655,000; Zorg en welzijn ~1,247,000; Technische beroepen ~1,107,000; Commerciële beroepen ~910,000; Dienstverlenende beroepen ~679,000; Transport en logistiek ~581,000 — [CBS StatLine 86355NED](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/cijfers/detail/86355NED)
- CBS distinguishes almost 440 occupations grouped into occupational classes; the detailed table is "Werkzame beroepsbevolking; beroep" — [CBS StatLine 86320NED](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/cijfers/detail/86320NED); [CBS dashboard Beroepen van werkenden](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/visualisaties/dashboard-arbeidsmarkt/werkenden/beroepen-van-werkenden)
- Among zzp'ers the largest occupational group is technical occupations: 224,000 in 2025 — [CBS Dossier ZZP](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/dossier/dossier-zzp)
- Open vacancies are concentrated in the same big sectors: trade, healthcare and business services together hold more than half of all open vacancies (CBS, Q1 2026 release) — [CBS, Minder vacatures in eerste kwartaal van 2026](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/18/minder-vacatures-in-eerste-kwartaal-van-2026)
- UWV sector pages (updated Nov 2026): the number of people in administrative occupations is fairly stable, but for administrative assistants and secretaries there has been steadily less work for years — [HRMorgen, UWV: 17 sectorpagina's arbeidsmarkt vernieuwd](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/11/uwv-17-sectorpaginas-arbeidsmarkt-vernieuwd)

### Inferences
- The occupations where ZZP contracting is common (technical/construction, care, transport) sit inside three of the six largest occupation classes, so the addressable pool of workers is large even though only a minority are self-employed.
- The 86355NED numbers understate technical and care headcount relevant to a ZZP marketplace because self-employed are excluded (224k technical zzp'ers come on top).

### Gaps
- No ranking of the top individual occupations (4-digit BRC, e.g. verkoopmedewerkers, laders/lossers, schoonmakers) with headcounts — the StatLine table could not be opened.
- No employment-by-sector (SBI) job totals for 2025/2026 were retrieved (jobs in zorg, handel, bouw, horeca etc.).

## 2. Which occupations have the most open vacancies and the tightest labour market (UWV krapteberoepen, Spanningsindicator, CBS vacancies) — numbers per sector

### Takeaway
At end-Q3 2026 there were about 375,000 open vacancies; the market is cooling slightly but UWV still rated 87 of 93 occupation groups (94%) as tight or very tight in Q1 2026. Construction has the highest vacancy rate of any sector (about 73 vacancies per 1,000 jobs), and the worst and still-worsening shortages are in technical and transport occupations.

### Cited Findings
**CBS vacancy counts**
- End of Q3 2026: 375,000 open vacancies, practically unchanged from Q2 2026; unemployment rose by 11,000 in the quarter; headline "tightness eases slightly" — [CBS, Krapte op de arbeidsmarkt neemt iets af](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/44/krapte-op-de-arbeidsmarkt-neemt-iets-af)
- Open vacancies by sector, Q3 2026, seasonally adjusted (x1,000): Handel ~70.7; Zorg ~69.1; Zakelijke dienstverlening ~61.7; Industrie ~29.9; Bouw ~28.4; Horeca 26.5 (Q2: 26.4); Informatie en communicatie 16.5 (Q2: 16.3); Vervoer en opslag 14.2 (Q2: 14.4) — [CBS, Krapte op de arbeidsmarkt neemt iets af](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/44/krapte-op-de-arbeidsmarkt-neemt-iets-af); [CBS StatLine 80474ned](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/cijfers/detail/80474ned)
- Third-party dashboards give slightly different sector numbers (horeca 25,000 or 29,000; transport 15,000) — treat CBS StatLine as authoritative — [dataradar.nl vacancy dashboard](https://www.dataradar.nl/dashboards/vacatures/)
- Vacancy rate (vacatures per 1,000 jobs): construction is the sector with the highest rate, 73 after a slight fall from 74; education is lowest at 18 (2026 CBS release; the extract did not make clear whether this is the Q1 or Q3 2026 release) — [CBS, Minder vacatures in eerste kwartaal van 2026](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/18/minder-vacatures-in-eerste-kwartaal-van-2026)
- Vacancy rate transport & storage: 37 per 1,000 jobs in Q3 2026 (CBS data via secondary site) — [checkitwithdata.com](https://checkitwithdata.com/branche/494-goederenvervoer-over-de-weg)
- Vacancy rate horeca: 54 per 1,000 jobs in Q2 2026 (secondary) — [radiushire.com](https://radiushire.com/nl/frontline/personeelstekort-nederland-werkvloer-cijfers)
- Tightness ratio: 91 vacancies per 100 unemployed in Q1 2026 — [CBS, Minder vacatures in eerste kwartaal van 2026](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/18/minder-vacatures-in-eerste-kwartaal-van-2026); a CBS regional page gives 101 per 100 for Q2 2026 vs 107 in Q2 2025 (not seasonally adjusted, so not comparable with the 91) — [CBS, Spanning op de arbeidsmarkt naar regio](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/visualisaties/dashboard-arbeidsmarkt/spanning-op-de-arbeidsmarkt/spanning-op-de-arbeidsmarkt-naar-regio)

**UWV Spanningsindicator / krapteberoepen**
- Definition: open vacancies divided by short-term jobseekers (people on WW benefit for under 6 months); computed for 93 occupation groups; dashboard refreshed on the first working day of March, June, September and December — [UWV Spanningsindicator dashboard](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/dashboards/spanningsindicator)
- Thresholds: value from 1.5 = tight ("krap"), from 4.0 = very tight ("zeer krap") (secondary sources; not confirmed on a UWV page in this session) — [HRMorgen](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/08/uwv-arbeidsmarkt-blijft-in-94-procent-van-de-beroepsgroepen-krap); [recruitmenttraining.pro](https://recruitmenttraining.pro/nieuws/schaarse-beroepen/)
- Q1 2026: 87 of 93 occupation groups (94%) still tight or very tight, although tension fell slightly in many occupations — [HRMorgen, UWV: arbeidsmarkt blijft in 94 procent van de beroepsgroepen krap](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/08/uwv-arbeidsmarkt-blijft-in-94-procent-van-de-beroepsgroepen-krap); [UWV, Spanningsindicator: arbeidsmarkt koelt verder af](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/spanningsindicator-arbeidsmarkt-koelt-verder-af)
- UWV + ROA: for 32 occupation groups the market is already (very) tight AND a further increase in tension is expected to 2030 — [UWV, Krapte neemt nog verder toe in 32 beroepen](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/krapte-neemt-nog-verder-toe-in-32-beroepen); underlying report dated 18 Dec 2025 — [UWV, Ontwikkeling krapte naar beroep 2024-2030 (PDF)](https://www.uwv.nl/assets-kai/files/3dadda9c-258e-4d9f-b60c-eac75496816b/ontwikkeling-krapte-naar-beroep-2024-2030.pdf)
- Occupations named in that group — technical: engineers, production managers industry/construction, machine mechanics (machinemonteurs), finishing construction workers (bouwarbeiders afbouw), car mechanics; transport: truck drivers, deck officers, pilots, bus drivers and tram drivers; also physiotherapists, nurses and cleaners (schoonmakers) — [Flexmarkt](https://www.flexmarkt.nl/brancheinformatie/uwv-verwacht-toenemende-arbeidskrapte-in-techniek-en-transport-tot-2030/); [Transport Online](https://www.transport-online.nl/110529/uwv-komende-jaren-grotere-arbeidskrapte-in-transport-en-techniek/); [HRMorgen Feb 2026](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/02/uwv-brengt-toekomstige-krapte-in-kaart-per-beroep/)
- Care: mixed picture — tension eases in some care/welfare occupations while shortages grow in others; in every province there is very large tightness for doctors, (physio)therapists and specialised nurses — [UWV, Krapte neemt nog verder toe in 32 beroepen](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/krapte-neemt-nog-verder-toe-in-32-beroepen)
- UWV publishes a "Kansrijke beroepen 2026-2027" list — [UWV PDF](https://www.uwv.nl/assets-kai/files/cb477fc7-719f-43ea-ac31-c15f428b864c/kansrijke-beroepen-2026-2027.pdf)
- Employers in construction, care and education most often expect more difficulty filling vacancies (secondary) — [workned.nl](https://workned.nl/krapte-arbeidsmarkt-2027/)

### Inferences
- Absolute vacancy volume is highest in trade, care and business services (roughly 60-70k each), but relative scarcity is highest in construction: ~28k vacancies on a much smaller job base gives a vacancy rate (73) about twice that of transport (37) and four times education (18).
- "bouwarbeiders afbouw" (finishing trades: plasterers, tilers, painters, floor layers) being singled out by UWV matches exactly the trades typically hired as ZZP.
- Cleaning appears in the UWV worsening-shortage list even though it is a low-skill occupation, i.e. scarcity is not limited to qualified trades.

### Gaps
- Exact Spanningsindicator values per occupation group (e.g. elektriciens, loodgieters, timmerlieden, vrachtwagenchauffeurs, ICT) were NOT retrieved; the UWV dashboard could not be opened. Whether electricians/plumbers/carpenters are among the 32 groups is unconfirmed.
- No CBS vacancy count for agriculture/greenhouses or for cleaning (cleaning sits inside "zakelijke dienstverlening").
- Q3 2026 vacancies-per-100-unemployed figure not captured.
- No Indeed / Jobdigger / Intelligence Group vacancy-by-occupation data found.

## 3. How many ZZP'ers are there, in which sectors, growth trend 2024-2026, and typical hourly rates per trade?

### Takeaway
CBS (labour-force survey, main job) counts about 1.2 million zzp'ers in 2025, down 62,000 (~5%) in one year and still falling to ~1.1 million by Q2 2026, while KVK's register count is far higher (1,825,347 on 30 Sep 2026) and has been rising again through 2026. Typical 2026 rates: construction trades roughly EUR 45-60/hour ex VAT, drivers about EUR 41, care EUR 70 (EUR 108 with a master's), IT about EUR 94-108, against an all-zzp average of about EUR 83.

### Cited Findings
**Counts and trend**
- CBS: 1.2 million zzp'ers in 2025, 62,000 fewer than 2024 (from nearly 1.3 million; about -5%), after years of growth — [CBS, Aantal zzp'ers in 2025 gedaald met 62 duizend](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/07/aantal-zzp-ers-in-2025-gedaald-met-62-duizend)
- Decline strongest among the young: 19,000 fewer zzp'ers aged 15-27 in 2025; in care alone about 5,000 fewer young zzp'ers — [CBS, Aantal zzp'ers daalt het sterkst onder jongeren](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/07/aantal-zzp-ers-daalt-het-sterkst-onder-jongeren)
- Q2 2026: zzp'ers down a further 15,000 to 1.1 million; all self-employed (with and without staff) almost 1.5 million, 88,000 fewer than a year earlier; fifth consecutive quarterly fall — [CBS, Werkloosheid gedaald in tweede kwartaal 2026](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/31/werkloosheid-gedaald-in-tweede-kwartaal-2026)
- Flex employees have been above year-earlier levels for three quarters in a row (2026) — [CBS, Al drie kwartalen meer flexwerknemers dan een jaar eerder](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/26/al-drie-kwartalen-meer-flexwerknemers-dan-een-jaar-eerder)
- KVK register: 1,825,347 zzp'ers registered on 30 Sep 2026, over 20,000 more than a quarter earlier; zzp starters +18.7% y/y and stoppers -6.2% y/y in Q3 2026; zzp'ers were 82% of all starters in Q3 2026 (79% in Q2) — [ZZP Nieuws](https://www.zzpnieuws.nl/zzpers-onder-starters-82-procent-q3-2026/); [ZiPconomy, KVK ziet aantal zzp'ers weer flink groeien (Jul 2026)](https://www.zipconomy.nl/2026/07/kvk-ziet-aantal-zzpers-weer-flink-groeien/)
- KVK Q2 2026: zzp starters +6% vs Q2 2025, stoppers -10.4% — [Flexnieuws](https://www.flexnieuws.nl/2026/07/kvk-ziet-weer-flinke-groei-aantal-startende-zzpers/)
- Definition spread: CBS ~1.13-1.2 million (zzp as main job) vs KVK ~1.77-1.83 million registrations; counting side-job zzp via registers CBS gets 1.95 million (2024, provisional) — [ZZP Nieuws kennisbank](https://www.zzpnieuws.nl/kennisbank/hoeveel-zzpers-nederland/); [bankformats.com](https://www.bankformats.com/nl/cijfers)

**Sectors**
- Care & welfare: zzp'ers rose from 89,000 (Q3 2015) to 141,000 (Q3 2025) = 8.2% of all workers in the sector — [CBS longread, Data over zelfstandigen in zorg en welzijn](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/aanvullende-statistische-diensten/2025/data-over-zelfstandigen-in-zorg-en-welzijn-trends-ondernemingen-klanten?onepage=true)
- Construction and care each had about 20,000 fewer zzp'ers in Q4 2025 than a year earlier (CBS figures combined with Loket.nl payroll data; SME employee numbers in these sectors grew faster than usual) — [Flexmarkt](https://www.flexmarkt.nl/brancheinformatie/afname-aantal-zzpers-gaat-in-meerdere-sectoren-samen-met-groei-van-personeel/); [ANP persportaal](https://persportaal.anp.nl/artikel/92cdd57a-91ec-44f1-8606-6fbd1e8e1538/afname-zzpers-gaat-in-verschillende-sectoren-samen-met-sterke-personeelsgroei)
- Earlier CBS release (mid-2025): zzp count in construction -8.4% in one year, care & welfare the same percentage — [ZiPconomy, Aug 2025](https://www.zipconomy.nl/2025/08/cbs-ziet-verdere-daling-aantal-zzpers-en-dat-komt-niet-alleen-door-handhaving/)
- KVK 2026 by sector: Q1 2026 construction zzp total -0.1% with starters +13% and stoppers -13%; in Q2 above-average extra starters in sports instruction (+20%), transport (+10.4%) and construction (+7.4%); care zzp numbers still falling in Q1 2026 and home care (thuiszorg) -2.6% in three months — [ZiPconomy, Apr 2026](https://www.zipconomy.nl/2026/04/kvk-ziet-aantal-zzpers-weer-licht-groeien/); [ZiPconomy, Jul 2026](https://www.zipconomy.nl/2026/07/kvk-ziet-aantal-zzpers-weer-flink-groeien/)
- KVK: the share of zzp'ers among all registered establishments is above average in education, construction and care — [KVK Trendrapport Q2 2026 (PDF)](https://production-site-nl.kvk.bloomreach.cloud/binaries/content/assets/kvkwebsite-nl/categorie/pers/kvk_trendrapportage_q2_2026.pdf)

- Ranges from a staffing blog (commercial source), 2026: schilder/stukadoor EUR 40-52; timmerman/metselaar EUR 45-58; loodgieter/elektricien EUR 50-65 — [Intro Personeel](https://www.intropersoneel.nl/blog/wat-is-een-goed-uurtarief-zzp-in-2026/)
- Stukadoor EUR 35-50; tegelzetter EUR 39 (undated calculator page) — [salaristabel.nl](https://salaristabel.nl/zzp-tarief-berekenen)
- Chauffeur (zzp): about EUR 41 on average, starters EUR 37, experienced EUR 44 — [Knab, zzp-chauffeur](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/wat-verdient-een-chauffeur-bekijk-het-uurtarief-en-de-winst); truck drivers EUR 35-60, specialised (ADR, refrigerated) EUR 50-75 — [hoebeginik.nl](https://hoebeginik.nl/zzp-vrachtwagenchauffeur/); a lower estimate of about EUR 30 also circulates — [kopvanbrabant.nl](https://kopvanbrabant.nl/artikelen/uurloon-zzp-chauffeur-6640.html)
- Care: EUR 70 without a master's degree, EUR 108 with one (Knab 2026) — [Knab, uurtarief zzp zorg 2026](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/uurtarief-zzpers-in-de-zorg)
- IT: IT consultant EUR 108, software developer EUR 94 (Knab 2026) — [MKB Servicedesk](https://www.mkbservicedesk.nl/nieuws/ondernemersnieuws/uurtarieven-van-zzpers-in-2026-tarieven-stijgen-verder-verschillen-blijven); other benchmark EUR 84 (range 68-100) — [zzpnettocheck.nl](https://www.zzpnettocheck.nl/zzp-uurtarief-benchmark)
- Horeca: EUR 49 (Knab via secondary) — [dezaak.nl](https://www.dezaak.nl/financien/administratie/zo-bepaal-je-als-zzper-je-uurtarief-een-stappenplan/)
- Rate floor: Rabobank cites a minimum hourly rate of EUR 38 as of 1 Jan 2026 in connection with the legal presumption of an employment contract; another blog cites about EUR 36 as the risk threshold — sources disagree, verify officially — [Rabobank](https://www.rabobank.nl/bedrijven/eigen-bedrijf-starten/financien/uurtarief-berekenen-zzp-2026); [Intro Personeel](https://www.intropersoneel.nl/blog/wat-is-een-goed-uurtarief-zzp-in-2026/)

### Inferences
- The CBS and KVK series move in opposite directions in 2026 (CBS still falling, KVK registrations rising). They measure different things (main-job labour-force status vs. Chamber of Commerce registrations, incl. side-job and dormant businesses), so "the number of ZZP'ers" must always be quoted with its source.
- KVK starter data suggest the supply shock is sector-specific: construction and transport ZZP inflow recovered in 2026, care (especially home care) keeps shrinking.
- Construction-trade rates (EUR 45-57) sit well below the all-zzp mean (EUR 83) and not far above the EUR 36-38 presumption threshold, so low-end construction/driver rates are the ones exposed to that rule.

### Gaps
- No CBS table of zzp'ers per sector (SBI) for 2025 was retrieved. An Ondernemersplein factsheet extract gave implausible sector values (e.g. "884,000 self-employed in specialist business services", "756,000 in education") that conflict with the ~1.5 million total — almost certainly a misread; not used.
- No reliable 2026 hourly rate for tegelzetter, stukadoor (only an undated calculator), or cleaning.
- Knab rates are self-reported by Knab customers, not a representative sample.

## 4. Which sectors rely most on ZZP and flexible/migrant labour, and what changed after Wet DBA enforcement resumed on 1 January 2025?

### Takeaway
Construction, care and education have the highest ZZP shares, while logistics, horticulture and food processing depend on agency-employed migrant workers. Since the Belastingdienst resumed enforcement on 1 Jan 2025 the CBS zzp count has fallen for five straight quarters, with construction and care each losing roughly 20,000 zzp'ers in a year and employees/flex contracts absorbing the work.

### Cited Findings
- The Belastingdienst resumed (stricter) enforcement on bogus self-employment on 1 Jan 2025; CBS links the 2025 fall in zzp'ers to it, and notes the labour market did not shrink — the number of employed rose in employee and flex contracts — [CBS, Aantal zzp'ers in 2025 gedaald met 62 duizend](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2026/07/aantal-zzp-ers-in-2025-gedaald-met-62-duizend); [Taxlive](https://www.taxlive.nl/nl/documenten/nieuws/aantal-jonge-zzpers-daalt-door-handhaving-schijnzelfstandigheid/)
- Clients in care, construction and technology became more cautious about hiring freelancers, and fewer people started as zzp from unemployment in 2025 (secondary) — [wbn.nl](https://wbn.nl/wijbusinessnieuws/aantal-zzpers-daalt-met-62-000-naar-12-miljoen-in-2025/)
- ZiPconomy (Aug 2025): the decline "is not only due to enforcement" — [ZiPconomy](https://www.zipconomy.nl/2025/08/cbs-ziet-verdere-daling-aantal-zzpers-en-dat-komt-niet-alleen-door-handhaving/)
- ESB: more inflow into employment after the bogus-self-employment crackdown — [ESB](https://esb.nu/meer-instroom-dienstverband-na-aanpak-schijnzelfstandigheid/)
- Assignments for highly educated zzp'ers down 42% since end-2024 (flex/intermediary market data, specific segment) — [MKB Servicedesk](https://www.mkbservicedesk.nl/nieuws/ondernemersnieuws/wet-dba-zorgt-voor-42-minder-opdrachten-voor-hoogopgeleide-zzpers); [Onderneming.nl](https://www.onderneming.nl/nieuws/onderzoeken/wet-dba-zorgt-voor-flinke-krimp-flexmarkt-minder-opdrachten-voor-hogeropgeleide-zzpers/)
- Penalties (sources not fully consistent; none is an official Belastingdienst page): no default fines (verzuimboetes) in 2025 or 2026; offence fines (vergrijpboetes) for intent/gross negligence possible from 1 Jan 2026; the "soft landing" ends 1 Jan 2027 — [KNOV](https://www.knov.nl/actueel/nieuws/nieuwsbericht/volledige-handhaving-wet-dba-uitgesteld); [freelance.nl](https://www.freelance.nl/community/wet-dba-2025)
- Legislative status: the first part of the Vbar bill was reportedly dropped by the cabinet in March 2026; one blog claims a new art. 7:610aa BW takes effect 31 Dec 2026 shifting the burden of proof to the client — unconfirmed by an official source — [SWV Accountants](https://www.swvaccountants.nl/post/wet-dba-2026-wat-betekent-de-handhaving-op-schijnzelfstandigheid-voor-jouw-branche); [thetalentrecruiters.nl](https://www.thetalentrecruiters.nl/wet-dba-2026-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers/)
- Sector exposure named by advisers: construction (about 20,000 zzp'ers lost), transport (zzp drivers common, extra risk), care (many institutions use zzp nurses/carers because of shortages), IT — [SWV Accountants](https://www.swvaccountants.nl/post/wet-dba-2026-wat-betekent-de-handhaving-op-schijnzelfstandigheid-voor-jouw-branche)
- KVK attributes the continuing fall of zzp'ers in care and culture to uncertainty over regulation and bogus self-employment — [KVK Trendrapport Q1 2026 (PDF)](https://production-site-nl.kvk.bloomreach.cloud/binaries/content/assets/kvkwebsite-nl/categorie/onderzoeken/0169-kvk-trendrapport-q1-2026-wr-pac.pdf)
- ABN AMRO: in construction (incl. contracting) just over half (52%) of workers are self-employed — partial extract, year and exact scope not visible — [ABN AMRO Sectorprognoses 2026-2027 (PDF)](https://www.metaalmagazine.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/ABN-AMRO-rapport-Sectorprognoses-2026-2027.pdf)
- Techniek Nederland forecast: in the installation sector the self-employed grow faster than employees, and "self-employed mechanics" account for almost 70% of the (additional) need — partial extract — [Techniek Nederland prognose tot 2030 (PDF)](https://www.technieknederland.nl/media/mpengwwh/prognose-van-de-werkgelegenheid-in-de-technische-installatiebranche-tot-2030-1.pdf)
- Migrant labour: agency-employed migrants by sector (NBBU, June 2022-June 2023): logistics 37%, horticulture 21%, food industry 18% — [NBBU](https://www.nbbu.nl/nl/arbeidsmigranten); Zuid-Holland (Decisio 2024): transport & logistics 38%, industry 26%, agriculture/horticulture 24% — [Provincie Zuid-Holland report](https://www.zuid-holland.nl/publish/besluitenattachments/reactie-motie-1503-bestuurlijke-visie-arbeidsmigratie/2-rapportage-birch-onderzoek-opgave-arbeidsmigratie-provincie-zuid-holland-def.pdf)
- Number of labour migrants: SEO (2026) extract says about 573,000 labour migrants, of whom over 315,000 agency workers, with an expected fall of 8.2% over 2024-2026 (10% in the agency sector) — [SEO, Arbeidsmigranten in de uitzendsector (PDF)](https://www.seo.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-41-Arbeidsmigranten-in-de-uitzendsector.-Onderzoek-naar-omvang-en-kenmerken.pdf); contradicted by higher figures elsewhere: 984,169 for 2022 (NBBU), "over 800,000" — [NBBU](https://www.nbbu.nl/nl/arbeidsmigranten); [HBP Media](https://hbpmedia.nl/arbeidsmigranten-cruciaal-voor-economie-wat-als-ze-vertrekken/)
- Greenhouse horticulture: 90-95% of agency workers in greenhouse horticulture and its chain are migrant workers (popular article, no clear primary source) — [welingelichtekringen.nl](https://www.welingelichtekringen.nl/economie/zonder-arbeidsmigranten-komt-de-nederlandse-oogst-in-gevaar-zo-afhankelijk-is-de-landbouw)

### Inferences
- Two different flexible-labour models coexist: skilled trades, care and IT are staffed through ZZP contracting; warehousing, greenhouses and food processing are staffed through agency employment of EU migrants, not ZZP. A ZZP order marketplace naturally fits the first group.
- Post-2025 the legal risk sits with the client, so demand shifted from long single-client ZZP placements (care shifts, drivers, IT secondment) to payroll/employment, whereas project-based trades work for multiple clients (construction finishing, installation) recovered in 2026 KVK starter data.
- The ~20,000 construction figure appears in both CBS-based reporting and adviser blogs, so it is probably the same underlying CBS number rather than two independent confirmations.

### Gaps
- No official Belastingdienst statement of the enforcement timetable/fine regime was read; dates above come from trade and adviser sites and conflict in detail.
- Kinderopvang (childcare) and horeca Wet DBA effects: nothing found.
- No current (2025-2026) official count of labour migrants by sector; the SEO 573k figure may use a narrower definition than NBBU's — not verified against the PDF.
- CBS ZZP share per sector (zzp as % of workers) only found for care (8.2%).

## 5. Which trades are so short that clients struggle to find workers, and where is supply plentiful?

### Takeaway
Clients struggle most to find technical and transport workers — engineers, machine and car mechanics, finishing-trade construction workers, installation technicians, truck/bus/tram drivers — plus nurses, physiotherapists and cleaners. Supply is comparatively plentiful in administrative and secretarial work, and jobs are shrinking in agency work, retail, wholesale, industry and financial services.

### Cited Findings
- Hardest to fill and worsening to 2030: engineers, production managers (industry/construction), machinemonteurs, bouwarbeiders afbouw, automonteurs, truck drivers, deck officers, pilots, bus and tram drivers, nurses, physiotherapists, cleaners — [UWV, Krapte neemt nog verder toe in 32 beroepen](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/krapte-neemt-nog-verder-toe-in-32-beroepen); [Flexmarkt](https://www.flexmarkt.nl/brancheinformatie/uwv-verwacht-toenemende-arbeidskrapte-in-techniek-en-transport-tot-2030/)
- Only 6 of 93 occupation groups were not tight in Q1 2026 — [HRMorgen](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/08/uwv-arbeidsmarkt-blijft-in-94-procent-van-de-beroepsgroepen-krap)
- Easing tension: several business-economic and administrative occupations, e.g. executive secretaries; less and less work for administrative assistants and secretaries — [UWV, Spanningsindicator: arbeidsmarkt koelt verder af](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/spanningsindicator-arbeidsmarkt-koelt-verder-af); [HRMorgen Nov 2026](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/11/uwv-17-sectorpaginas-arbeidsmarkt-vernieuwd)
- UWV/ROA (Dec 2025): for administrative assistants the market stays tight in indicator terms despite expected job losses, because replacement demand is high — [UWV, Ontwikkeling krapte naar beroep 2024-2030 (PDF)](https://www.uwv.nl/assets-kai/files/3dadda9c-258e-4d9f-b60c-eac75496816b/ontwikkeling-krapte-naar-beroep-2024-2030.pdf)
- UWV notes that many vacancies does not always mean tightness — [UWV, Achter de cijfers: veel vacatures, geen krapte](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/achter-cijfers-veel-vacatures-geen-krapte)
- Installation sector: under-25s leave quickly — almost half leave within one year — [Installatie.nl](https://www.installatie.nl/nieuws/installatiebranche-heeft-121-000-nieuwe-technici-nodig/)
- Grid operators and their contractors: shortage of about 28,000 technicians to 2029 (Netbeheer Nederland, via press release) — [persberichten.com](https://www.persberichten.com/persbericht/115588/28-000-technici-tekort-wat-gebeurt-er-met-de-kennis-van-ervaren-monteurs)
- UWV also publishes a study on European supply for Dutch shortage occupations (28 May 2026) — [UWV PDF](https://www.uwv.nl/assets-kai/files/21e163e5-9d5b-45e0-929f-16dd83a2d818/europees-aanbod-voor-nederlandse-tekortberoepen-28-5-2026.pdf)
- Knab reports construction zzp'ers in 2026 have plenty of work, healthy profits and confidence (headline only) — [Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/bouw-zzpers-in-2026-veel-werk-gezonde-winsten-en-vertrouwen-in-de-toekomst)

### Inferences
- "Easy to staff" in the Netherlands is relative: even retail and horeca still post 26-70k vacancies. The genuinely loose segments are office/administrative support, not manual work.
- For a client, the scarcest hires are licensed/certified technical roles (installation, electrical, mechanics) and drivers; unskilled warehouse/greenhouse labour is supplied in bulk through migrant agency channels rather than being scarce at the individual level.

### Gaps
- No quantified indicator values to rank trades (which is tighter: electricians vs plumbers vs carpenters).
- The six non-tight occupation groups in Q1 2026 were not listed in any extract.
- No data on time-to-fill or share of hard-to-fill vacancies per trade.

## 6. Forecasts 2026-2030 (UWV, CPB, ABN AMRO, ING, EIB, Techniek Nederland)

### Takeaway
Forecasters expect a slowly cooling but still tight market: CPB sees unemployment at 3.9% in 2026 and 4.0% in 2027, UWV expects 1.7% job growth 2025-2028 with agency work shrinking. Construction and installation face the largest structural hiring needs — EIB about 75,000 new full-time workers for 2026-2029, the installation sector about 121,000 new workers for 2025-2029.

### Cited Findings
- CPB MEV 2027 (Sept 2026): unemployment 3.9% in 2026 and 4.0% in 2027 (March forecast was 4.1% and 4.3%); GDP growth 1.4% in 2026, 1.2% in 2027; employment decline continues in the forecast years and labour supply grows more slowly — [AWVN, MEV 2027](https://www.awvn.nl/nieuws/mev-2027-wat-werkgevers-ervan-moeten-weten/); [CPB raming MEV 2027 (PDF via AWVN)](https://www.awvn.nl/app/uploads/2026/09/CPB-raming-Macro-economische-verkenning-2027.pdf); [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2626820)
- UWV Arbeidsmarktprognose 2026-2028: national job growth 1.7% between 2025 and 2028 (assumes an early end to the war in the Middle East); strongest growth in specialist business services, care & welfare, information & communication; job shrinkage in industry, retail, wholesale, financial services; agency sector shrinks (about -4.1% in a regional edition); vacancies expected to fall in 2026 and 2027 — [HRMorgen, Aug 2026](https://www.hrmorgen.nl/2026/08/arbeidsmarktprognose-uwv-krapte-blijft-in-veel-sectoren/); [UWV Arbeidsmarktprognose 2026-2028 (PDF)](https://www.uwv.nl/assets-kai/files/98e81276-a408-4cb2-b3c7-d793f988d5b1/uwv-arbeidsmarktprognose-2026-2028.pdf); [Flexmarkt](https://www.flexmarkt.nl/brancheinformatie/arbeidsmarkt-koelt-iets-af-maar-blijft-krap-personeelstekort-remt-cruciale-sectoren/)
- Earlier UWV edition (2025-2027): total job growth 1.1% to 2027; care & welfare +7.2%; agencies -3.0% — [UWV, Arbeidsmarktprognose: beperkte banengroei tot 2027](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/trends-ontwikkelingen/arbeidsmarktprognose-beperkte-banengroei-tot-2027); [technischwerken.nl](https://www.technischwerken.nl/nieuws/uwv-verwacht-kleine-groei-in-het-aantal-banen-tot-2027/)
- UWV/ROA to 2030: staff shortages rise mainly in technical and transport occupations — [Transport Online](https://www.transport-online.nl/110529/uwv-komende-jaren-grotere-arbeidskrapte-in-transport-en-techniek/)
- EIB (Jan 2026): construction output +2.5% in 2026 and +4.5% in 2027; output in constant prices from EUR 103 bn (2025) to EUR 118 bn (2030); needs over 30,000 extra workers (definition truncated in extract) — [EIB persbericht Verwachtingen bouwproductie en werkgelegenheid 2026 (PDF)](https://www.bouwenmetstaal.nl/sites/default/files/2026-01/EIB_Persbericht_Verwachtingen%20bouwproductie%20en%20werkgelegenheid%202026.pdf)
- EIB (Oct 2025), Trends op de bouwarbeidsmarkt 2025-2029: 75,000 new full-time workers needed in 2026-2029; ~50,000 can come from education, 25,000 must come via lateral entry; output growth ~3%/year; employment +~25,000 labour-years plus 50,000 replacements for retirement/disability — [EIB persbericht (PDF)](https://www.eib.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/276-Persbericht-Trends-op-de-bouwarbeidsmarkt-2025-2029.pdf); [EIB rapport (PDF)](https://www.eib.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/EIB-rapport-Trends-op-de-bouwarbeidsmarkt-2025-2029.pdf)
- English-language report: construction needs over 73,000 extra workers by 2030 (2027-2030), ~52,000 from new graduates — [NL Times](https://nltimes.nl/node/92640)
- Installation sector (Wij Techniek / KBA Nijmegen): about 121,000 new workers needed 2025-2029, largely because about 118,000 leave; employment grows ~0.5%/year from 2026 after a 2025 dip — [Installatie.nl](https://www.installatie.nl/nieuws/installatiebranche-heeft-121-000-nieuwe-technici-nodig/); [Techniek Nederland prognose (PDF)](https://www.technieknederland.nl/media/mpengwwh/prognose-van-de-werkgelegenheid-in-de-technische-installatiebranche-tot-2030-1.pdf)
- Techniek Nederland Monitor Bouwketen spring 2026 repeats the EIB +2.5% for 2026 — [Techniek Nederland (PDF)](https://www.technieknederland.nl/media/pe2gdgfr/monitor-bouwketen-voorjaar-2026-definitief.pdf)
- ABN AMRO construction: volume +2.5% in 2026 and 2027 in one edition; another piece gives turnover +2% (2026) and +3% (2027), with labour shortages named as a structural brake despite full order books — [ABN AMRO, Gematigde groei bouw ondanks volle orderboeken](https://www.abnamro.nl/nl/zakelijk/insights/sectoren-en-trends/sectorprognoses/bouw/gematigde-groei-bouw-ondanks-volle-orderboeken.html)
- ABN AMRO transport & logistics (Sectorprognoses 2027-2028, Nov 2026): +1.0% in 2026, +1.5% in 2027, +1.0% in 2028; recovering Dutch construction and German infrastructure spending add freight demand; AI and robotisation gaining ground — [TTM.nl](https://www.ttm.nl/management/abn-amro-verwacht-gematigde-groei-transport-en-logistiek-ai-en-robotisering-winnen-terrein/180804/)

### Inferences
- The EIB numbers (30,000 / 73,000 / 75,000) are not comparable: one is net expansion demand, the others include replacement of leavers and cover different periods. For the report, "about 75,000 new full-time workers 2026-2029, of which 25,000 beyond what education supplies" is the cleanest statement.
- With agency employment forecast to shrink and construction/installation needing 25,000+ lateral entrants, demand for directly contracted skilled trades should stay strong through 2030 even as the overall market cools.
