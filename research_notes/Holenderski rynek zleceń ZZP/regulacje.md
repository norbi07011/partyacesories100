## 1. Wet DBA: requirements, end of the moratorium (1 Jan 2025), soft landing, 2026 enforcement, model agreements, inspection numbers

### Takeaway
Enforcement on false self-employment has been fully "on" since 1 January 2025, but the fine regime has been phased: no fines in 2025, no default fines (verzuimboetes) in 2026 either, offence fines (vergrijpboetes) possible again from 1 January 2026, and the last soft-landing elements end on 1 January 2027. Back-tax assessments (naheffingen) for working relations from 1 January 2025 onward have been possible throughout, so the real financial risk sits with the client, and it already exists.

### Cited Findings
- Since 1 January 2025 the Belastingdienst checks whether self-employed workers actually work independently; fines for 2025 were waived, but back-tax assessments remained possible for work from 1 January 2025. — [Rijksoverheid, 18 Dec 2024](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/zelfstandigen-zonder-personeel-zzp/nieuws/2024/12/18/in-2025-geen-boetes-bij-handhaving-schijnzelfstandigheid); [MKB Servicedesk](https://www.mkbservicedesk.nl/nieuws/ondernemersnieuws/belastingdienst-voert-ruim-800-controles-uit-op-schijnzelfstandigheid-bij)
- End of December 2025 the cabinet only partially extended the soft landing for 2026: no verzuimboetes are imposed in 2026 for false self-employment. The Tweede Kamer had asked for more (motions Ergin c.s. and Martens-America/Vermeer of 18 Dec 2025). — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.nl/insights/legal-services/wet-dba/kabinet-verlengt-zachte-landing-bij-aanpak-schijnzelfstandigheid-gedeeltelijk-in-2026/); [Accountancy Vanmorgen, 19 Dec 2025](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2025/12/19/kabinet-zwicht-voor-kamerdruk-ook-in-2026-geen-verzuimboetes-bij-schijnzelfstandigheid/); [Belastingdienst Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties)
- From 1 January 2026 the Belastingdienst can again impose vergrijpboetes; for corrections relating to 2025 no fine is imposed. — [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/handhaving-schijnzelfstandigen-vanaf-tweeduizendzesentwintig.html)
- In 2026 the Belastingdienst in principle always starts with a company visit (bedrijfsbezoek) rather than a full audit; a boekenonderzoek is required for a wage-tax naheffing and remains possible in 2026. — [Salaris Vanmorgen, 7 Jan 2026](https://www.salarisvanmorgen.nl/2026/01/07/handhaven-in-2026-met-gedeeltelijke-verlenging-zachte-landing/); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/nl/en/who-we-are/news-events-and-publications/news/handhaving-op-zzp-ers-vanaf-1-januari)
- The remaining soft-landing elements lapse on 1 January 2027; regular verzuimboetes then become possible again. — [HR Praktijk](https://www.hrpraktijk.nl/juridisch/wet-dba/pauze-op-zzp-boetes-kabinet-verlengt-coulante-handhaving-schijnzelfstandigheid-tot-eind-2026/)
- The Handhavingsplan arbeidsrelaties 2026 exists in v1.0 (11 Dec 2025) and v2.0 (31 Dec 2025). Enforcement on working relations is not yet part of regular enforcement plans in 2026; the aim is to integrate it from 2027. Enforcement is not targeted at specific sectors unless sampling shows a distinction is useful. — [Belastingdienst handhaving](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/handhaving); [Dirkzwager](https://www.dirkzwager.nl/kennis/artikelen/nieuw-handhavingsplan-arbeidsrelaties-2026-wat-betekent-dit-voor-opdrachtgevers-en-zzpers)
- Numbers: 842 company visits and 237 boekenonderzoeken concerning self-employed workers, "through November of this year". — [MKB Servicedesk](https://www.mkbservicedesk.nl/nieuws/ondernemersnieuws/belastingdienst-voert-ruim-800-controles-uit-op-schijnzelfstandigheid-bij) (single secondary source; the year, most likely 2025, could not be confirmed)
- Four months into stricter enforcement, problems were concentrated in healthcare. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2565865)
- Model agreements: since 6 September 2024 the Belastingdienst assesses no new model agreements and extends none. Agreements approved and valid on 6 September 2024 may be used until 31 December 2029, whatever date is printed on them. They give certainty only if the parties actually work as described. — [Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/arbeidsrelaties/content/geen-nieuwe-modelovereenkomsten-meer)
- Published general model agreements include "geen werkgeversgezag" (VNO-NCW/MKB-Nederland) and "tussenkomst" (intermediary). — [Belastingdienst: geen werkgeversgezag](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/voorbeeldovereenkomst_geen_werkgeversgezag); [Belastingdienst: tussenkomst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/themaoverstijgend/brochures_en_publicaties/voorbeeldovereenkomst_tussenkomst)

### Inferences
- A marketplace launching in 2027 enters a market where clients face naheffing plus verzuimboete plus vergrijpboete. Client fear of hiring ZZP'ers is therefore likely to rise again in 2027, which is both a demand risk and an opening for a platform that documents independence per assignment.
- A platform cannot obtain its own approved model agreement any more. It can only reuse an existing general one (e.g. "tussenkomst" or "geen werkgeversgezag") until end-2029, and that covers the contract text only, not actual practice.
- System error: the fiscal test is open-norm and retrospective; the only ex-ante instrument (model agreements) is being phased out without a replacement before the Zelfstandigenwet arrives.

### Gaps
- Official 2026 figures on visits, audits, correction obligations and naheffingen were not found. They are probably in the voortgangsbrief "werken met en als zelfstandigen" (Kamerstuk 31 311) — not accessible in this session.
- The exact definition of which soft-landing elements end on 1 Jan 2027 was seen only in secondary sources.
- Background (unverified here): the Wet DBA (2016) abolished the VAR and makes client and contractor jointly responsible for the correct qualification; the client bears the wage-tax and social-premium consequences.

## 2. Case law: Deliveroo (HR 24 March 2023), Uber (HR 21 Feb 2025; Hof Amsterdam Jan 2026), Temper (Hof Amsterdam 16 June 2026), Helpling

### Takeaway
Dutch courts use a holistic "all circumstances" test with nine non-ranked viewpoints (Deliveroo); entrepreneurship of the individual worker counts fully and can tip the balance (Uber), so outcomes are individual. The decisive warning for a marketplace is Temper: in June 2026 the Amsterdam Court of Appeal reclassified a ZZP shift platform as a temp agency, because the platform recruited, selected, controlled access, set quality rules and could exclude workers.

### Cited Findings
- Deliveroo (ECLI:NL:HR:2023:443, 24 March 2023): the Hoge Raad upheld that Deliveroo riders worked under an employment contract; the freedom to be replaced and the freedom not to work did not prevent that. It formulated a non-exhaustive list of nine circumstances. — [NJB](https://www.njb.nl/nieuws/hoge-raad-bezorgers-deliveroo-zijn-geen-zelfstandigen/); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.nl/insights/themas/legal/hoge-raad-in-deliveroo-arrest-bezorgers-hebben-een-arbeidsovereenkomst/)
- The nine viewpoints fall into three groups: who has the power to determine (bepaalmacht), whether the work is embedded in the organisation (inbedding), and the nature of work, worker and remuneration. The Hoge Raad left clarification of "inbedding" to the legislator. — [Stibbe annotation](https://www.stibbe.com/nl/publications-and-insights/het-deliveroo-arrest-de-ontzagging-van-de-arbeidsovereenkomst-opgelost); [Dirkzwager](https://www.dirkzwager.nl/kennis/artikelen/deliveroo)
- Uber (ECLI:NL:HR:2025:319, 21 Feb 2025, preliminary questions): there is no ranking between the Deliveroo circumstances; "entrepreneurship" is not less important than the others; circumstances outside the specific relationship (external entrepreneurship) are relevant too. The same driver population can contain both employees and entrepreneurs. — [Accountancy Vanmorgen](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2025/02/21/hoge-raad-geeft-uitleg-deliveroo-arrest-ondernemerschap-kan-doorslaggevend-zijn/); [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2556813)
- Uber, Hof Amsterdam (January 2026): FNV's claims that all drivers or groups of drivers are employees were rejected; the six drivers who joined on Uber's side are independent entrepreneurs; assessment must be individual. — [Rechtspraak.nl](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/gerechtshoven/gerechtshof-amsterdam/nieuws/2026/01/vorderingen-fnv-in-uber-zaak-afgewezen); [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/hrnuanceertinUberkwestiededeliverooindicaties.html)
- Temper, first instance (Rechtbank Amsterdam, 10 July 2024, ECLI:NL:RBAMS:2024:3987): no uitzendovereenkomst between Temper and workers. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2528289); [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/nieuws/rechtbank-temper-geen-uitzendbureau)
- Temper, appeal (Hof Amsterdam, 16 June 2026, ECLI:NL:GHAMS:2026:1612): reversed. Temper workers are agency workers under art. 7:690 BW and Temper is a temp agency; the temp-agency CAO applies. Reasons reported: Temper recruits and selects, controls access to jobs via the app, sets quality requirements, can exclude workers and sets the platform rules, so it is not a mere intermediary; workers work for Temper's account and risk; the factoring payment arrangement did not change this. Back payment was ordered of the €1 per hour workers paid Temper until 2019. — [Rechtspraak.nl](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/gerechtshoven/gerechtshof-amsterdam/nieuws/2026/06/werkers-van-temper-zijn-uitzendkrachten); [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2618890); [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/hof_amsterdam_merkt_temper_aan_als_uitzendonderneming.html); [Loyens & Loeff](https://www.loyensloeff.com/nl/insights/news--events/news/gerechtshof-amsterdam-temper-werkers-kwalificeren-als-uitzendkrachten/)
- Commentators link Temper to the "shared authority" (gedeeld gezag) reasoning of the Helpling case; the ruling rests on Temper's specific design and does not automatically apply to other platforms. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2618890); [Rendement](https://www.rendement.nl/werken-met-zzp-ers/nieuws/werkenden-via-temper-zijn-toch-uitzendkrachten.html)
- Helpling: a Hoge Raad judgment of 11 April 2025 exists (referenced only). — [ZiPconomy paper](https://www.zipconomy.nl/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/De-rol-van-externe-ondernemerschap_mr-JJW-van-Ladestijn.pdf)

### Inferences
- Two separate reclassification routes threaten a platform: (a) worker = employee of the client; (b) worker = agency worker of the platform itself (Temper/Helpling route). Route (b) is the existential one, because it brings the temp CAO, back pay, and the Wtta admission duty.
- Platform features the Temper court counted against the platform: selection/vetting, gating access to jobs, quality rules, exclusion power, platform-set rules, payment flow through the platform. Many of these are also what DSA/KYC and client trust require — a structural tension.
- After Uber, real entrepreneurship of the individual (several clients, own tools and investment, own acquisition, VAT registration, liability insurance) is the strongest defence, and a platform can collect evidence of it.

### Gaps
- Whether Temper filed cassation with the Hoge Raad: not found.
- Full text of the Temper and Uber appeal judgments not read; reasoning taken from summaries.
- Content of the Helpling Hoge Raad judgment not retrieved.
- Background (unverified here): the nine Deliveroo viewpoints are commonly listed as nature and duration of work; how work and hours are determined; embedding in the organisation; duty of personal performance; how the contract terms came about; how pay is determined and paid; level of pay; commercial risk; and whether the worker behaves as an entrepreneur.

## 3. Vbar, legal presumption at an hourly rate, Zelfstandigenwet: what the coalition decided in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The clarification half of the Vbar bill is dead (withdrawn by cabinet decision of 6 March 2026); the legal-presumption half was split off, passed, and enters into force on 31 December 2026. A government Zelfstandigenwet, modelled on the VVD/D66/CDA/SGP initiative bill, is to replace the clarification part, but is not expected in the Tweede Kamer before summer 2027.

### Cited Findings
- 7 July 2025: the Vbar bill was sent to the Tweede Kamer. — [Rijksoverheid](https://rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/07/07/wetsvoorstel-voor-meer-duidelijkheid-zzpers-en-sterkere-positie-laagbetaalde-schijnzelfstandigen-naar-de-kamer)
- 27 March 2025: a Kamerbrief announced that Vbar would be adapted to the Hoge Raad's Uber ruling. — [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/hoge-raad-en-politiek-geven-nadere-duiding-in-zzp-discussie)
- 6 March 2026: the cabinet withdrew the clarification part of Vbar (it would have entered into force on 1 July 2026) and announced a separate Zelfstandigenwet, taking the principles of the VVD/D66/CDA/SGP initiative bill as guideline; the legal presumption is kept and accelerated. — [Rijksoverheid, 6 March 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/03/06/kabinet-kiest-voor-meer-rust-en-duidelijkheid-voor-zzpers-en-opdrachtgevers); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.nl/insights/legal-services/wet-dba/zzp-wetgeving-in-beweging-verduidelijkingsdeel-vbar-vervalt-kabinet-werkt-aan-nieuwe-zelfstandigenwet/); [Accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2026/3/kabinet-zet-streep-door-groot-deel-van-nieuwe-zzp-wet/)
- The "Wet invoering rechtsvermoeden van arbeidsovereenkomst op basis van uurtarief" (dossier 36 783) was adopted by the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026, is dated 18 June 2026, published in Staatsblad 2026, 158, and enters into force on 31 December 2026. — [Eerste Kamer dossier 36783](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36783_wet_invoering); [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/vanaf-31-december-2026-arbeidsovereenkomst-bij-uurtarief-onder-38); [CMS, Prinsjesdag 2026](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/flexibiliteit-wordt-minder-vrijblijvend-wat-prinsjesdag-2026-betekent-voor-werkgevers-in-nederland)
- Threshold: most sources say an hourly rate below €38 (price level 1 January 2026); the Staatsblad text is reported as "ten hoogste €36 per uur", with the applicable amount at first application set by ministerial regulation. — [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/flexibiliteit-wordt-minder-vrijblijvend-wat-prinsjesdag-2026-betekent-voor-werkgevers-in-nederland); [Staatsblad 2026, 158](https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stb-2026-158.odt)
- Effect: when the worker invokes the presumption, the burden of proof shifts to the client, who may prove there is no employment contract. It works in civil law only; UWV, Belastingdienst and Arbeidsinspectie do not apply it. — [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/flexibiliteit-wordt-minder-vrijblijvend-wat-prinsjesdag-2026-betekent-voor-werkgevers-in-nederland); [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/kabinetskoers-zelfstandigen-focus-op-wat-wel-kan.html)
- Zelfstandigenwet timing: the bill is to be sent to the Tweede Kamer before summer 2027. — [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/flexibiliteit-wordt-minder-vrijblijvend-wat-prinsjesdag-2026-betekent-voor-werkgevers-in-nederland)

### Inferences
- The €36 vs €38 difference is probably statute amount versus indexed amount (indexation to 1 Jan 2026 price level); the binding figure is whatever the ministerial regulation sets for 31 Dec 2026. This is an inference and must be verified.
- Product implication: from 31 Dec 2026 every assignment priced under roughly €36-38 per hour carries a reversed burden of proof for the client. In construction, logistics and services with many Polish ZZP'ers, a large share of hourly rates sits near or under that line. A platform that defaults to project/result pricing still needs an hourly equivalent, because the presumption is measured per hour.
- A visible rate floor or warning at the threshold is a cheap risk signal for clients, but it is not a safe harbour: above the threshold the ordinary Deliveroo test still applies.
- System error: until at least 2028 there is no statutory ex-ante certainty (no VAR, no new model agreements, no Zelfstandigenwet), while enforcement and a presumption are both live.

### Gaps
- Exact threshold amount at entry into force (€36 or €38) and the ministerial regulation: not confirmed.
- How the hourly rate is computed for fixed-price jobs, and whether costs/materials are excluded: not found.
- Content of the government Zelfstandigenwet (zelfstandigentoets, werkrelatietoets, sector presumptions), any internet consultation, and the fate of the initiative bill itself: not confirmed in this session.

## 4. EU Platform Work Directive (2024/2831) and the Dutch Wet platformwerk

### Takeaway
The Netherlands will miss the 2 December 2026 transposition deadline: the draft Wet platformwerk was only in internet consultation from 29 June to 24 August 2026 and had not been reported as submitted to parliament. The draft gives platform workers a presumption of employment against the platform when 2 of 5 control criteria are met, plus algorithmic-management duties that apply regardless of contract type.

### Cited Findings
- The directive must be transposed by 2 December 2026. — [Taxence](https://www.taxence.nl/nieuws/nieuwe-wet-moet-platformwerkers-beter-beschermen-en-speelveld-gelijktrekken/)
- Draft Wet platformwerk published for internet consultation on 29 June 2026, open until 24 August 2026. — [Rijksoverheid, 29 June 2026](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/06/29/gelijker-speelveld-en-betere-positie-platformwerker-door-nieuwe-wet); [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/wetsvoorstel-wet-platformwerk-betere-arbeidsvoorwaarden-platformwerkers)
- Legal presumption: a platform worker can invoke it when two of five statutory criteria are met; the platform must then prove there is no employment contract. The test is holistic, like the Deliveroo viewpoints. — [Rendement](https://www.rendement.nl/werken-met-zzp-ers/nieuws/wetsvoorstel-beschermt-arbeidsrechten-platformwerkers.html)
- Criteria as reported: the platform determines or influences the level of remuneration; the platform allocates assignments; the platform supervises the work; the freedom to refuse assignments is restricted; the platform sets binding rules on appearance, conduct or performance. — [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/wetsvoorstel-wet-platformwerk-betere-arbeidsvoorwaarden-platformwerkers); [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/06/29/gelijker-speelveld-en-betere-positie-platformwerker-door-nieuwe-wet)
- Algorithmic management: important decisions such as termination or suspension may only be taken by a human; workers get insight into how algorithms set prices and distribute assignments. — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/06/29/gelijker-speelveld-en-betere-positie-platformwerker-door-nieuwe-wet)
- Scope: work organised through a digital labour platform, irrespective of contract form (employee or self-employed). — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/06/29/gelijker-speelveld-en-betere-positie-platformwerker-door-nieuwe-wet)
- Consultation criticism: VZN (self-employed association) says the criteria are so broad that normal business arrangements with self-employed people could be caught, and asks for clarity on which platforms, intermediaries and brokers fall under the act. — [ZZP Nederland](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/comment/333)

### Inferences
- A marketplace matching clients with ZZP'ers through an app is very likely a "digital labour platform" in the directive's sense. The algorithmic-management duties (transparency, human review of suspension/termination, explanation) will apply even if every user is a genuine entrepreneur.
- The five criteria read as a design checklist in reverse: do not set or influence price, do not allocate jobs, do not supervise, do not penalise refusals, do not prescribe conduct or appearance. Meeting two is enough to shift the burden of proof to the platform.
- After 2 December 2026, with no Dutch act in force, courts may interpret Dutch law in conformity with the directive; whether a worker could rely on it directly against a private platform is doubtful. This is a legal inference, not a sourced finding.

### Gaps
- The exact five criteria in the draft text, and the definition of "digitaal arbeidsplatform" (in particular whether pure listing/lead-generation sites are excluded): not read in the primary text; sources phrase the criteria differently.
- Status after the consultation (Raad van State, submission to the Tweede Kamer, target date of entry into force): not found.
- Relationship between the 2-of-5 platform presumption and the hourly-rate presumption: not found.

## 5. Wtta: admission system for lenders of labour; mediation vs. terbeschikkingstelling vs. aanneming van werk; WAADI

### Takeaway
The Wtta admission system starts on 1 January 2027, with enforcement of the lending and hiring bans from 1 January 2028. Pure ZZP mediation is in principle outside it, but only as long as the worker is not placed under the client's direction and supervision — and after Temper, a shift-style ZZP platform can be requalified as a lender (uitlener), which pulls it under the Wtta.

### Cited Findings
- The admission system takes effect on 1 January 2027; the Nederlandse Autoriteit Uitleenmarkt (NAU) assesses applications. Enforcement by the Arbeidsinspectie of the lending and hiring ban starts on 1 January 2028. — [SRA, July 2026](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/gefaseerde-inwerkingtreding-wet-toelating-ter-beschikking-stelling-arbeidskrachten-wtta); [Randstad](https://www.randstad.nl/werkgevers/hr-wet-en-regelgeving/aannemen-en-contracten/personeel-aannemen/wtta-waadi); [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/wtta-strengere-regels-voor-uitzendbureaus-en-andere-uitleners/)
- Transitional regime: only lenders that have registered by 31 December 2026 fall under the transitional arrangement; companies that lend labour only to a very limited extent can apply for an exemption from 1 May 2027. — [SRA, July 2026](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/gefaseerde-inwerkingtreding-wet-toelating-ter-beschikking-stelling-arbeidskrachten-wtta)
- Deposit (waarborgsom): €100,000 per legal entity as the main rule; start-ups pay €50,000 first and another €50,000 after six months; refunded after four years of compliance. — [ABU practical guide](https://www.abu.nl/app/uploads/2026/04/Praktische-gids-klaar-voor-de-Wtta-ABU.pdf); [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/gefaseerde-inwerkingtreding-wet-toelating-ter-beschikking-stelling-arbeidskrachten-wtta)
- Scope and ZZP: the duty applies where workers are deployed under the direction and supervision of another; a genuine self-employed person does not fall under it; lenders of ZZP'ers generally do fall under the system. — [ABU guide](https://www.abu.nl/app/uploads/2026/04/Praktische-gids-klaar-voor-de-Wtta-ABU.pdf); [Freelance.nl](https://www.freelance.nl/community/wtta); [SRA, Oct 2026](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/011102/2026/10/val-jij-onder-het-nieuwe-toelatingsstelsel-van-de-wtta)
- Certain cases of ZZP mediation can be classified as terbeschikkingstelling of labour. — [Stibbe](https://www.stibbe.com/sites/default/files/2025-09/bb-2025_47.pdf)
- Exemptions: non-profit collegial lending and intra-group lending are outside the system. — [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/gefaseerde-inwerkingtreding-wet-toelating-ter-beschikking-stelling-arbeidskrachten-wtta)
- The Wtta replaces the WAADI registration duty with the admission system; other WAADI rules (e.g. equal treatment) remain. A client that hires from a non-registered lender can be fined. — [Randstad](https://www.randstad.nl/werkgevers/werkpocket/wet-en-regelgeving/waadi-en-wtta)

### Inferences
- Three legal shapes, in order of regulatory weight: (1) bemiddeling — the platform brings parties together and they contract directly; no Wtta, no agency CAO; (2) terbeschikkingstelling — the worker works under the client's direction and supervision via the platform; Wtta admission, €100k deposit, WAADI equal-pay rules, from 2028 a hiring ban for clients using a non-admitted lender; (3) aanneming van werk — the contractor delivers a result under its own direction; outside Wtta but inside chain liability.
- Clients (inleners) will from 2028 be exposed to fines if they hire through a non-admitted lender, so they will demand proof either of admission or of genuine mediation/aanneming. A platform that can show a clean mediation model has a selling point; one in the grey zone becomes unusable for cautious clients.
- For the target sectors, result-based assignments (aanneming van werk: "tile this bathroom", "install these 12 heat pumps") fit both the Wtta carve-out and the DBA test better than "8 hours of hands on site X".

### Gaps
- No government source giving a bright-line rule for when a ZZP platform is an uitlener; this is fact-dependent.
- Wtta fine amounts, the full admission standards (e.g. VOG, housing rules), and the legal instrument fixing the dates (possibly Staatsblad 2026, 159) were not read.
- Whether the 31 December 2026 registration deadline is still open or was adjusted: single source.

## 6. Chain liability, hirer's liability, G-rekening, Wet arbeid vreemdelingen identity checks, Wkb

### Takeaway
A genuine ZZP'er without staff creates little wage-tax chain risk for the contractor, but the risk reappears the moment the ZZP'er is requalified or passes work on; and the Wav treats whoever actually has work done as "employer", so identity checks apply to ZZP'ers too. These rules are stable law, not 2026 novelties.

### Cited Findings
- Chain liability: a contractor that subcontracts stays jointly liable for wage tax and premiums down the chain; a ZZP'er who in turn outsources to a party with personnel (subcontractor, temp or payroll firm) triggers it. — [Ondernemen met personeel](https://www.ondernemenmetpersoneel.nl/mijn-branche/bouw/ketenaansprakelijkheid); [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/ketenaansprakelijk-heid-en-een-g-rekening/)
- G-rekening: a blocked account into which the wage-tax/premium part of an invoice is paid; a ZZP'er pays no wage tax for himself, so a G-rekening is not always needed for a ZZP'er. — [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/ketenaansprakelijk-heid-en-een-g-rekening/)
- Chain liability for wages also exists (Wet aanpak schijnconstructies). — [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/ketenaansprakelijkheid-voor-loon-voorkomen/)
- Wav: broad employer concept — whoever has work actually performed is responsible, regardless of payroll; fines can hit several links in the chain for one illegally working person. — [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/legal-updates/een-illegale-medewerker-meerdere-boetes-de-risico-s-van-ketenaansprakelijkheid)
- Wav fine amounts per a 2018 article: €1,500 for breach of the identity-check duty (art. 15 Wav); €8,000 per illegally employed foreign national, €12,000 in aggravated cases, cumulative per person. — [Kroes Advocaten / TBR 2018](https://www.kroesadvocaten.nl/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/ketenaansprakelijkheid_bij_bestuurlijke_boetes_voor_illegale_tewerkstelling.pdf) (old source; current amounts not verified)

### Inferences
- Polish and other EU nationals need no work permit, but the client must still establish and record identity; a platform can take that friction away with one verified ID check that is reusable across clients. This is a clear product opportunity, subject to GDPR limits on copying ID documents and BSN.
- For ZZP'ers with non-EU nationality holding EU residence documents (e.g. Ukrainians, Belarusians living in Poland) the Wav risk is real: a Polish residence permit does not by itself give the right to work as a self-employed person in the Netherlands. This is an inference that needs legal confirmation.
- System error: a main contractor cannot tell from the outside whether a "ZZP'er" brings helpers; liability then arises without the contractor knowing.

### Gaps
- Wkb (Wet kwaliteitsborging voor het bouwen): no source retrieved in this session on its implications for ZZP hired via a platform. Background (unverified): the Wkb has applied since 1 January 2024 to gevolgklasse 1 new-build; it extends contractor liability for defects after delivery and requires a consumer dossier, which increases the main contractor's need to document who did what — relevant for a platform's job records.
- Inlenersaansprakelijkheid (hirer's liability, applicable to lending, and relevant if a platform is requalified as uitlener) was not separately sourced.
- Current Wav fine levels and current Belastingdienst guidance on G-rekening percentages: not verified.

## 7. Requirements for a foreign/EU national to work as ZZP (BSN, KvK, VAT, KOR, A1, WagwEU notification, VCA)

### Takeaway
Two distinct routes exist and platforms often mix them up: (a) a ZZP'er established in the Netherlands (BSN, KvK, Dutch VAT-id, Dutch tax and social insurance), and (b) a self-employed person established in Poland working temporarily in the Netherlands (Polish business, A1 from ZUS, and a mandatory pre-start notification in the Dutch posted-workers portal for listed sectors including construction).

### Cited Findings
- Self-employed persons from the EU/EEA/Switzerland with a temporary assignment in the Netherlands must notify via the Dutch online portal before starting, in designated sectors; construction, cleaning and the food industry are among them. — [Government.nl](https://government.nl/topics/foreign-citizens-working-in-the-netherlands/foreign-companies%E2%80%99-duty-to-report/foreign-employers-and-self-employed-persons-with-a-temporary-assignment-in-the-netherlands); [Postedworkers.nl factsheet for self-employed](https://www.postedworkers.nl/binaries/postedworkers/documenten/publicaties/2023/08/09/factsheet-buitenlandse-zzper-engels/WagWEU_Factsheet+ZZP%27ers_Engels.pdf)
- The notification covers identity, company details, client details, SBI activity code, workplace address, expected start and end dates, and an A1 form or other proof of where social contributions are paid. — [Postedworkers.nl factsheet](https://www.postedworkers.nl/binaries/postedworkers/documenten/publicaties/2023/08/09/factsheet-buitenlandse-zzper-engels/WagWEU_Factsheet+ZZP%27ers_Engels.pdf)
- The Dutch client has its own duties when contracting a foreign business (checking the notification). — [Government.nl](https://www.government.nl/topics/foreign-citizens-working-in-the-netherlands/foreign-companies%E2%80%99-duty-to-report/dutch-companies-contracting-a-foreign-business)
- A1: a self-employed person temporarily working in another EU country needs an A1 certificate, applied for in the home country. — [Business.gov.nl](https://business.gov.nl/regulation/a1-statement); [SVB](https://svb.nl/en/bbz-bdz/you-are-self-employed/you-are-going-to-work-in-another-country-temporarily)
- KOR: national small-business VAT exemption with a €20,000 annual turnover limit; exceeding it during the year ends the scheme. Since 1 January 2025 an EU-KOR exists: maximum €100,000 total EU turnover and staying under the national limit of the member state where it is used. — [KVK belastingtarieven 2026](https://www.kvk.nl/geldzaken/belastingtarieven-2026/)

### Inferences
- A platform should force a choice at onboarding between "NL-established" and "PL-established, temporary" and collect different documents for each: KvK + Dutch VAT-id vs. CEIDG/NIP + A1 + notification number. The client-side check of the notification can be automated.
- A Polish-established ZZP'er working for long stretches in the Netherlands for few clients is a weak case on three fronts at once: A1 validity (temporary, normally up to 24 months), Dutch tax residence/permanent establishment, and the DBA/entrepreneurship test.
- Under the KOR a ZZP'er invoices without VAT, which is attractive for private (consumer) clients; the €20,000 cap means it only fits part-timers.

### Gaps
- BSN and KvK registration steps for an EU national (RNI registration for non-residents, KvK appointment, automatic VAT-id issue), not sourced in this session. Background (unverified): KvK registration triggers assessment by the Belastingdienst for VAT and income-tax entrepreneurship; a BSN is obtained through municipal registration (BRP) or as a non-resident via an RNI desk.
- VCA and trade certificates: no source retrieved. Background (unverified): VCA is contractual/market-driven (required by most main contractors), not a statutory licence; certain work is certificate-bound (gas-fired installations since the CO-certification regime, asbestos, F-gases, some electrical inspection work).
- Recognition of Polish professional qualifications and diplomas: not researched.
- Exact ZUS procedure and criteria for an A1 for self-employed (substantial prior activity in Poland): not confirmed.

## 8. Compulsory disability insurance (Wet BAZ), zelfstandigenaftrek, other 2026-2028 changes

### Takeaway
The fiscal advantage of being a ZZP'er keeps shrinking (zelfstandigenaftrek €2,470 in 2025, €1,200 in 2026, €900 in 2027) while a compulsory public disability insurance with opt-out is in parliament (bill 36 912, submitted 20 March 2026) with a start date reported as around 2030 and still uncertain.

### Cited Findings
- Wet basisverzekering arbeidsongeschiktheid zelfstandigen (BAZ), dossier 36 912, was submitted to the Tweede Kamer on 20 March 2026; a committee report was adopted on 29 May 2026. — [Eerste Kamer dossier 36912](https://www.eerstekamer.nl/wetsvoorstel/36912_wet_basisverzekering); [NJB](https://www.njb.nl/wetgeving/wetsvoorstellen/basisverzekering-arbeidsongeschiktheid-zelfstandigen/)
- Covered: self-employed persons reporting profit from business for income tax; DGAs and resultaatgenieters are outside. Public insurance by default with a conditional opt-out to private insurance; transitional rules for existing private policies that meet conditions. — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/hervormingen-arbeidsmarkt/meer-zekerheid-voor-werkenden/verplichte-arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering-voor-zelfstandigen); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/nl/nl/wie-zijn-wij/nieuws-events-en-publicaties/nieuws/verplichte-aov-zelfstandigen-in-aantocht)
- KVK presents the compulsory AOV as coming in 2030 ("Verplichte AOV in 2030, kies nu nog zelf"). — [KVK](https://www.kvk.nl/wetten-en-regels/de-verplichte-aov-voor-ondernemers-uitgelegd/)
- Zelfstandigenaftrek: €2,470 in 2025, €1,200 in 2026, €900 in 2027. — [MKB Servicedesk](https://www.mkbservicedesk.nl/nieuws/ondernemersnieuws/zelfstandigenaftrek-2024-verlaagd); [KVK belastingtarieven 2026](https://www.kvk.nl/geldzaken/belastingtarieven-2026/)
- MKB-winstvrijstelling stays at 12.7% in 2026. — [Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/dit-gebeurt-er-met-de-mkb-winstvrijstelling?hsLang=nl)
- Timeline of the other changes (see sections 1, 3, 4, 5): hourly-rate presumption 31 Dec 2026; full fines 1 Jan 2027; Wtta admission 1 Jan 2027 and enforcement 1 Jan 2028; Zelfstandigenwet to parliament before summer 2027; model agreements end 31 Dec 2029.

### Inferences
- 2027-2030 is a period of falling net advantage and rising compliance cost for low-rate ZZP'ers. Supply of ZZP'ers at the bottom of the rate range is likely to shrink or shift to employment/temp work; ZZP'ers with real specialisms and rates well above €38 are barely affected.
- Whether a Polish-insured ZZP'er (A1 from ZUS) would fall under the BAZ is unclear; EU coordination rules suggest not, since they remain in the Polish system. Inference only.

### Gaps
- BAZ parameters (premium percentage, benefit level, waiting period, premium ceiling), the Tweede Kamer plenary vote and the official commencement date: not found in this session.
- MKB-winstvrijstelling percentage for 2027 and any 2027 Belastingplan measures specific to entrepreneurs: not found.
- Other 2026-2028 items (pension access for ZZP, reforms of the urencriterium): not researched.

## 9. Obligations of the marketplace itself: DAC7, DSA, P2B, GDPR, consumer law (ACM), payments (PSD2)

### Takeaway
Independently of labour law, the marketplace is a regulated intermediary: it must report its service providers' earnings annually under DAC7, verify business users (DSA trader traceability), give 30 days' reasoned notice before terminating a business user (P2B), and must not hold client money itself without a payment licence or a licensed PSP.

### Cited Findings
- DAC7: a platform operator must collect, verify and report data on sellers, lessors and service providers to the Belastingdienst, including the tax identification number (BSN or RSIN). Data must be collected and verified by 31 December and reported by 31 January of the following year. — [Belastingdienst DAC7](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/ondernemers/content/informatie-voor-platformexploitanten-dac7); [Taxlive](https://www.taxlive.nl/nl/documenten/nieuws/platformexploitanten-informeren-fiscus-voor-1-februari-over-verkopers/)
- DAC7: a time-based or task-based activity is always to be treated as a "personal service" (so in scope). "Excluded platform operator" status requires proving that no reportable seller has access to the platform. — [Belastingdienst DAC7](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/ondernemers/content/informatie-voor-platformexploitanten-dac7)
- DAC7 enforcement on smaller platforms has been limited while an alternative reporting method for small platforms was developed; compliance among small platforms lags. — [SRA, Jan 2025](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/000000/2025/01/alternatieve-rapportagemethode-dac7-kleine-platformen); [Taxlive](https://www.taxlive.nl/nl/documenten/nieuws/beperkte-handhaving-dac7-rapportageplicht-blijft-bij-kleinere-platformen-vaak-achter/)
- DSA: ACM has supervised DSA obligations for services established in the Netherlands since 4 February 2025 and has published a DSA guideline; platforms that let consumers conclude contracts with traders must ensure traceability of those traders. ACM reports that many companies do not yet comply. — [ACM DSA guideline](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/acm-publiceert-leidraad-dsa-voor-aanbieders-online-diensten); [ACM](https://www.acm.nl/nl/publicaties/acm-veel-bedrijven-voldoen-nog-niet-aan-europese-digitale-wetgeving)
- P2B Regulation: ACM has been competent to supervise it since 13 November 2024; terms must be easily available to business users, and termination of the service to a business user requires a statement of reasons at least 30 days in advance. — [ACM online platforms](https://www.acm.nl/nl/digitale-economie/online-diensten-aanbieden/online-platforms); [ACM P2B guideline](https://www.acm.nl/system/files/documents/leidraad-platform-to-business-verordening.pdf)
- Consumer law: the ACM Leidraad Bescherming Online Consument (2024) covers ranking of search results and reviews; unreliable reviews can be an unfair commercial practice under Book 6 BW. — [ACM Leidraad 2024](https://www.acm.nl/system/files/documents/leidraad-bescherming-online-consument-2024.pdf); [ACM: recommendations and advertising on platforms](https://www.acm.nl/nl/digitale-economie/rechten-van-ondernemers-bij-online-diensten/aanbevelingen-en-reclame-op-online-platforms)
- PSD2: transactions through a commercial agent acting for only the payer or only the payee are not payment services; an agent acting for both sides is a payment service provider. DNB requires a written agreement, real negotiation or conclusion of the sale, and a relationship that goes beyond facilitating payment. — [DNB](https://www.dnb.nl/en/sector-information/open-book-supervision/open-book-supervision-sectors/payment-institutions/licensing-requirement-for-payment-service-providers-overview/payment-services-not-requiring-authorisation/); [DNB on e-commerce platforms under PSD2](https://www.dnb.nl/en/sector-information/supervision-laws-and-regulations/laws-and-eu-regulations/psd2/electronic-trading-platforms-e-commerce-platforms-under-psd2)
- In a typical marketplace model the platform cannot itself receive payments owed by buyers to sellers; PSD3 is expected to narrow the commercial-agent exemption further. — [Stripe guide](https://stripe.com/en-cz/guides/how-psd2-impacts-marketplaces-and-platforms); [Stripe on PSD3](https://stripe.com/en-cz/guides/what-platforms-and-marketplaces-can-expect-from-psd3) (vendor source with a commercial interest)

### Inferences
- A marketplace serving both client and ZZP'er cannot use the commercial-agent exemption, so escrow/split payments must run through a licensed PSP (Mollie, Stripe Connect, Online Payment Platform or similar) where the PSP, not the platform, holds the funds.
- Tension with labour law: the Temper court treated the platform's payment/factoring arrangement and its vetting and exclusion powers as signs of a temp agency, while DSA, DAC7 and AML-style PSP onboarding require the platform to verify, collect tax IDs and sometimes suspend users. The defensible position is to do the legally mandated identity/tax verification, document that it is mandated, and keep it separate from quality selection or work allocation.
- P2B and the platform-work rules point the same way on deactivation: reasons, notice, human review. Building one deactivation procedure that satisfies both is efficient.
- DAC7 makes ZZP earnings on the platform visible to the Dutch and (through exchange) the Polish tax authorities. This removes the grey-economy appeal for some users, and is a selling point to honest ones.

### Gaps
- DAC7 fine amounts and whether any de minimis exclusion applies to service providers: not confirmed. Background (unverified): the "fewer than 30 sales and under €2,000" exclusion applies to sale of goods only, not to personal services.
- DSA specifics (art. 30 trader traceability data set, micro/small enterprise exemption from the online-platform section, notice-and-action, point of contact): not read in primary text.
- GDPR: no source retrieved. Background (unverified): the BSN may only be processed where a statute requires it (DAC7 gives such a basis for reporting); copying ID documents is restricted; ratings/profiles and automated decisions fall under art. 22 GDPR alongside the platform-work rules.
- ACM rules on verified reviews and disclosure of ranking parameters and paid placement: guideline located but content not extracted.
- PSD3/PSR adoption status and dates: not confirmed.

## 10. Which platform designs are legally safer

### Takeaway
No design is a safe harbour, because every test is holistic, but case law and the draft Wet platformwerk point consistently at the same levers: the platform must not set price, allocate work, supervise, sanction refusals or prescribe conduct, and the worker must contract directly with the client as a demonstrable entrepreneur — ideally for a defined result rather than for hours under the client's direction.

### Cited Findings
- Draft Wet platformwerk criteria (2 of 5 triggers the presumption): platform determines/influences pay; allocates assignments; supervises the work; restricts freedom to refuse; sets binding rules on appearance, conduct or performance. — [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/07/wetsvoorstel-wet-platformwerk-betere-arbeidsvoorwaarden-platformwerkers); [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/06/29/gelijker-speelveld-en-betere-positie-platformwerker-door-nieuwe-wet)
- Temper (June 2026): recruiting and selecting, controlling access to jobs, quality requirements, power to exclude and platform-set rules made the platform more than an intermediary; the payment (factoring) arrangement did not help. — [Rechtspraak.nl](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/gerechtshoven/gerechtshof-amsterdam/nieuws/2026/06/werkers-van-temper-zijn-uitzendkrachten); [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/hof_amsterdam_merkt_temper_aan_als_uitzendonderneming.html)
- Deliveroo: contractual freedom of substitution and freedom not to work did not prevent employee status. — [NJB](https://www.njb.nl/nieuws/hoge-raad-bezorgers-deliveroo-zijn-geen-zelfstandigen/)
- Uber: the worker's entrepreneurship, including circumstances outside the relationship, counts fully and is assessed individually. — [Accountancy Vanmorgen](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2025/02/21/hoge-raad-geeft-uitleg-deliveroo-arrest-ondernemerschap-kan-doorslaggevend-zijn/); [Rechtspraak.nl, Uber appeal](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/gerechtshoven/gerechtshof-amsterdam/nieuws/2026/01/vorderingen-fnv-in-uber-zaak-afgewezen)
- Algorithmic decisions on suspension/termination must be taken by a human under the draft act. — [Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/06/29/gelijker-speelveld-en-betere-positie-platformwerker-door-nieuwe-wet)
- Hourly-rate presumption applies below roughly €36-38 per hour from 31 Dec 2026. — [CMS](https://cms.law/nl/nld/publication/flexibiliteit-wordt-minder-vrijblijvend-wat-prinsjesdag-2026-betekent-voor-werkgevers-in-nederland)
- Wtta applies where workers are placed under another's direction and supervision. — [ABU guide](https://www.abu.nl/app/uploads/2026/04/Praktische-gids-klaar-voor-de-Wtta-ABU.pdf)

### Inferences
Safer design choices (all inferences from the findings above):
- Price: the ZZP'er quotes; the platform shows no suggested rate, sets no minimum or maximum, and takes its fee as a transparent commission or subscription, preferably from the client side. A neutral warning at the legal-presumption threshold is information, not price-setting.
- Matching: the client posts a job and ZZP'ers respond with their own offer (tender/quote model), instead of the platform dispatching or ranking by an opaque algorithm. Ranking parameters are disclosed (also needed for P2B and consumer law).
- Scope of work: result-based assignments (aanneming van werk or a defined opdracht) with the ZZP'er deciding how, with own tools, materials and liability insurance, rather than hourly shifts filled on site under a foreman. Shift filling is the Temper pattern.
- Substitution: real, used in practice and not subject to platform approval; paper-only substitution clauses did not help Deliveroo.
- No sanctions: no penalties for declining, cancelling or low acceptance; no automated deactivation; reasons, notice and human review for any removal.
- Entrepreneurship evidence: record KvK, VAT-id, business insurance, several clients on and off the platform, own website/brand; allow and encourage off-platform client relationships (no exclusivity, no non-circumvention penalties aimed at workers).
- Contract chain: the client and ZZP'er contract directly; the platform is a party only to its own terms of use; the platform does not invoice in its own name for the work.
- Verification: limit vetting to legally mandated checks (identity, tax ID for DAC7, trader data for DSA, PSP onboarding) and factual, verifiable credentials (VCA, certificates) displayed for the client to judge, instead of platform-defined quality standards or admission tests.
- Money: licensed PSP holds funds; milestone/escrow release is triggered by the client, not by the platform's judgment of the work.

Riskier patterns: platform-set hourly rates; shift marketplaces for unskilled on-site labour; ratings that feed automatic access restrictions; mandatory platform-wide conduct rules or clothing; long, full-time engagements with one client at rates under the threshold.

System errors that make honest cooperation hard:
- No ex-ante certainty: VAR abolished, model agreements frozen until 2029, Zelfstandigenwet not before 2027-2028, while naheffingen run from 2025 and fines from 2026/2027.
- Three presumptions and tests with different triggers and different addressees (DBA fiscal test, civil hourly-rate presumption that the tax office ignores, 2-of-5 platform presumption) can give contradictory answers for the same job.
- Trust mechanisms are counted against the platform: vetting, quality rules, exclusion of bad actors and safe payment are what clients and EU platform law want, yet Temper treats them as agency indicators.
- The hourly-rate threshold penalises fixed-price and low-margin trades and cross-border workers whose cost base is lower, without measuring independence.
- Polish-established self-employed must notify each assignment in designated sectors and carry an A1, while the client has a parallel checking duty; small clients and consumers are generally unaware.
- The Wtta draws the line at "direction and supervision", the same vague concept that the DBA debate has failed to pin down for a decade; from 2028 clients risk fines for guessing wrong.

### Gaps
- No court or regulator has published a positive checklist for a compliant ZZP marketplace; the list above is derived by inversion from negative cases and draft criteria.
- How Dutch quote/tender platforms for tradespeople (lead-generation model) have been treated by courts or the Belastingdienst: not researched; no case found in this session.
- ZiPconomy, FD and law-firm analyses of post-Temper platform redesigns (H2 2026): not retrieved.
