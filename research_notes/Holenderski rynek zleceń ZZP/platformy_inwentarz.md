# Inventory of Dutch online marketplaces where ZZP'ers and small contractors find jobs (state: 2025 - Nov 2026)

> Method note for the report writer. Direct page fetches were blocked in this research session (the platforms' own sites, zzp-nederland.nl and others could not be opened), so every finding below comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from a full read of the page. Exact prices were confirmed only where a platform's own page was quoted in the result. Where a price is from a third party, or could not be found at all, this is stated. Nothing here was verified by logging into a platform as a tradesman. Items I know only from background knowledge and could not source in this session are listed under "Gaps", labelled as unverified.

## 1. Home-services / construction lead platforms: how does each charge the tradesman?

### Takeaway
The home-services segment runs almost entirely on pay-per-lead (the tradesman pays for the consumer's contact details, whether or not he wins the job), sometimes combined with a subscription; Zoofy is the exception with a fixed-price, pay-through-platform model. Werkspot (Instapro Group / Angi) is the market leader, and the Solvari/Offerte.nl/Casius group is the main consolidated challenger; most platforms other than Werkspot and Homedeal do not publish their prices for professionals.

### Cited Findings

**Werkspot (Instapro Group, part of the Angi family)**
- Werkspot is part of Instapro Group and the Angi family (Angi/HomeAdvisor). — [Werkspot over-ons, via search summary](https://www.werkspot.nl/over-ons)
- Costs are paid by the tradesman, not the consumer. — [Werkspot FAQ](https://www.werkspot.nl/blog/faq)
- Official lead price: shown before the tradesman responds, ranging from EUR 3.00 to EUR 75.00, fixed once he has responded. — [Werkspot FAQ](https://www.werkspot.nl/blog/faq-brandende-vragen-over-werkspot)
- Werkspot's terms define the "Leadprijs" as the fee owed when the customer's contact details are shared with the tradesman after his response; the terms also refer to subscriptions and packages as separate agreements, and Werkspot may change terms with 30 days' notice. — [Werkspot algemene voorwaarden](https://www.werkspot.nl/algemene-voorwaarden)
- A Werkspot promotion returned "the basic subscription value of EUR 240" as credit (undated promotion), which implies a basic subscription worth about EUR 240. — [Werkspot blog, cashback actie](https://www.werkspot.nl/blog/cashback-zomeractie-nu-tijdelijk-240-gratis-tegoed-bij-abonnement)
- Third-party (competitor) figures, unverified: Basic membership EUR 29.95/month and Premium EUR 49.95/month; lead ranges quoted elsewhere as EUR 6-40 or EUR 4-100. — [Slimster, a competitor](https://slimster.nl/blog/werkspot-alternatief/); [Adaptoo](https://adaptoo.nl/en/?p=8874)
- Scale: "market leader in the Netherlands", more than 350,000 jobs posted per year and more than 8,500 affiliated tradesmen (over-ons page, year of the figure unclear). — [Werkspot over-ons](https://www.werkspot.nl/over-ons)
- Scale, 2025: 45,000 clients per month hire tradesmen via Werkspot, based on jobs from January to December 2025. — [Werkspot kwaliteitscontrole](https://www.werkspot.nl/kwaliteitscontrole)
- Other Werkspot pages show cumulative counters of 6,736,006 jobs and about 40,494-41,525 tradesmen; these conflict with the 8,500 figure and are probably defined differently (all-time registered vs. active). — [Werkspot vakmannen page](https://www.werkspot.nl/uitbouw-renovatie/aannemer-vakmannen?page=1717); [Werkspot over-ons](https://www.werkspot.nl/over-ons)
- Audience: mainly private (consumer) clients; tradesmen in construction, installation, maintenance and home improvement. — [ZZP Nederland comparison](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/waar-vind-je-als-zzper-opdrachten-vergelijk-de-beste-marktplaatsen)

**Homedeal**
- Pure pay-per-lead: the price varies per quote request, there are no subscription costs, and the company pays only for the quote requests it unlocks. — [Homedeal for companies](https://www.homedeal.nl/bedrijven/)
- Offers a free trial; a Homedeal blog says the first fourteen days are free and positions it for construction ZZP'ers (promotional source). — [Homedeal blog, zzp opdrachten](https://www.homedeal.nl/bedrijven/blog/zzp-opdrachten)
- Homedeal runs company sign-up pages in Dutch, English, German and Italian, which indicates a multi-country operation. — [Homedeal Unternehmen](https://www.homedeal.nl/unternehmen); [Homedeal aziende](https://www.homedeal.nl/aziende)

**Solvari / Offerte.nl / Casius (one group)**
- Solvari bought Offerte.nl in spring 2024, its sixth acquisition in three years; Vortex Capital Partners has backed Solvari since 2018. — [Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i37325/solvari-koopt-offerte-nl-zesde-overname-in-3-jaar-tijd); [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/solvari-acquires-offerte-nl/)
- Offerte.nl connects consumer requests with local and regional businesses; it expanded to Belgium in 2021 and Scandinavia in 2023. — [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/solvari-acquires-offerte-nl/)
- Solvari acquired Casius and Twizzi in 2022; together those two had mediated over EUR 2 billion of building and renovation projects for private individuals in the Benelux since 2000. — [Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2022/03/22/solvari-reaches-for-the-hammer-nails-down-casius-and-twizzi-acquisitions)
- Solvari also acquired the Flemish Offrea and Power Leaf Group (zonnepanelen.net). — [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/wire/solvari-breidt-pool-vakmensen-uit-overname-vlaamse-offrea); [Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i24067/solvari-neemt-power-leaf-group-van-zonnepanelen-net-over)
- Solvari pricing for professionals, third-party only: an "Essential" package at EUR 49 per month plus credit bundles. Not confirmed on Solvari's own site. — [European Purpose comparison](https://europeanpurpose.com/nl/categorie/europese-huisdienstenmarktplaatsen)
- Conflict: one search summary stated that "Solvari was taken over by Offerte.nl in 2024". The primary press coverage says the reverse, Solvari bought Offerte.nl. — [Solar Magazine](https://solarmagazine.nl/nieuws-zonne-energie/i37325/solvari-koopt-offerte-nl-zesde-overname-in-3-jaar-tijd)

**Zoofy**
- Works with a fixed, pre-shown price for the customer ("Uber model"); the customer pays Zoofy by QR code or payment link, and Zoofy pays the professional once the customer is satisfied. — [Zoofy blog](https://zoofy.nl/en/blog/the-best-home-services-platform-in-the-netherlands/); [Zoofy about](https://zoofy.nl/en/about-us)
- Zoofy prepares the invoices for completed jobs on the professional's behalf. — [Zoofy terms for professionals](https://zoofy.nl/en/terms-and-conditions-for-pro)
- All jobs accepted through Zoofy must be paid entirely through Zoofy, including repeat work for the same customer up to 24 months after the last job; breaches lead to fines. — [Zoofy, agreements and preventing fines](https://zoofy.nl/en/service-professional/news/agreements-and-preventing-fines)
- A 2021 Zoofy post says "the largest part" of the amount goes to the professional and "a small part" to Zoofy, the customer pays a service fee, and professionals are paid twice a month. — [Zoofy news for professionals](https://zoofy.nl/en/service-professional/news/de-voordelen-van-zoofy-voor-de-zzp-er)
- March 2026: optional "Fast Payment" service at 2.95% per invoice. — [Zoofy Fast Payment Service](https://zoofy.nl/en/service-professional/news/zoofy-fast-payment-service)
- Anecdotal only: a Trustpilot reviewer claimed Zoofy "takes about 30% margin on the work"; Zoofy replied that the platform needs to make some money. Not an official rate. — [Trustpilot, Zoofy](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zoofy.nl?page=7)

**Trustoo**
- Consumer-facing "Top 10" comparison of local professionals per city and trade (plumbers, electricians, contractors, but also SEO specialists, graphic designers, advertising agencies). — [Trustoo example page](https://trustoo.nl/zuid-holland/leiden/loodgieter)
- No public price per lead found; a user testimonial on Trustoo's own site speaks of "low costs, quality leads", and leads arrive by e-mail or app push notification. — [Trustoo ervaringen](https://trustoo.nl/ervaringen/ervaring-websterk/)
- A comparison source states that seven of eleven home-services platforms do not publish prices for professionals. — [European Purpose](https://europeanpurpose.com/nl/categorie/europese-huisdienstenmarktplaatsen)

**Skydreams**
- Runs online quote sites for tradespeople; described as operating 36 individual lead-generation sites, and financed an acquisition from its own funds. — [MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/financiering/skydreams-financiert-overname-uit-eigen-zak)

**Bobex (Belgian, context)**
- Describes itself as a "dating site" for supply and demand; consumers and businesses request quotes free of charge. One of the largest quote-comparison sites for construction and renovation in the Benelux; it took over Verbouwkosten BV. — [Bobex](https://www.bobex.be/nl-be/over-bobex/); [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/nieuws/bobex-neemt-sectorgenoot-verbouwkosten)

**Slimster**
- A lead platform that markets itself explicitly as a "Werkspot alternative" for companies. — [Slimster](https://slimster.nl/bedrijven/aanmelden/werkspot-alternatief)

**Marktplaats "Diensten en Vakmensen"**
- Marktplaats has a dedicated category "Diensten en Vakmensen" with sub-categories such as "Klussers en Klusbedrijven". — [Marktplaats](https://www.marktplaats.nl/l/diensten-en-vakmensen/)
- Placing an ad is free under "Marktplaats Standaard", or paid per ad in paid categories; services is named as one of the categories where a small per-ad fee applies. — [Marktplaats business bundles](https://www.marktplaats.nl/smb-profile/bundles); [Shopify blog](https://www.shopify.com/nl/blog/verkopen-op-marktplaats)
- A business bundle was listed at EUR 49.95 per month after a 4-week 70% discount, cancellable monthly (possibly an older version of the page). — [Marktplaats business bundles](https://www.marktplaats.nl/smb-profile/bundles)

### Inferences
- The tradesman carries the acquisition risk on every lead platform except Zoofy: he pays for contact details, not for won work. On Zoofy the risk moves to a margin on executed work plus a lock-in on repeat customers.
- Consolidation is real: Solvari, Offerte.nl, Casius, Twizzi, Offrea and zonnepanelen.net sit under one owner, so several "different" quote sites feed the same lead pool.
- Price opacity is a structural feature of the segment. Only Werkspot (range) and Homedeal (model) publish anything; a Polish or other newcomer cannot compare costs before signing up.

### Gaps
- Werkspot's current subscription price per month and contract length were not confirmed on an official page; the only figures are a competitor's (EUR 29.95 / 49.95) and an undated EUR 240 promo. How many tradesmen may respond to one job was not found.
- No lead prices found for Homedeal (amounts), Solvari, Offerte.nl, Casius, Trustoo, Skydreams or Bobex.
- Zoofy's standard commission percentage is not published in any source found.
- Nothing found on Offerteadviseur, Klusup, Bouwmatcher, Klusidee: no ownership, pricing or scale data. Their current status is unverified.
- Nextdoor Netherlands (business pages, neighbourhood recommendations): no results on pricing or scale.
- No revenue figures for Werkspot/Instapro Group and no traffic estimates (Similarweb) for any platform; the Similarweb connector was unavailable.
- Unverified background, not sourced here: Skydreams is commonly associated with Homedeal as the same Breda-based group. This should be checked before use.

## 2. Gig / shift platforms: fees, sectors, status after Wet DBA enforcement and court rulings

### Takeaway
The shift-platform model is under direct legal attack: on 16 June 2026 the Amsterdam Court of Appeal ruled that Temper is a temporary employment agency and its workers are agency workers, though the ruling is not enforceable while Temper appeals to the Supreme Court. Helpling's Dutch arm has been bankrupt since January 2023, and from the end of 2026 a legal presumption of employment applies to work below a set hourly rate, which hits exactly the price range these platforms operate in.

### Cited Findings

**Temper**
- Client usage fee: EUR 4.40 per hour; lowest minimum worker rate EUR 15.50 per hour, with different minimums per job category. — [Temper help centre](https://help.temper.works/en/articles/4592679-for-clients-this-is-how-temper-works)
- A comparison article puts the all-in entry-level cost at about EUR 20.90 per hour on Temper against EUR 20.95 on YoungOnes. — [Quasa](https://quasa.io/nl/media/youngones-of-temper-eur005-verschil-maar-het-hof-noemt-temper-uitzendwerk)
- 16 June 2026: the Gerechtshof Amsterdam ruled that Temper qualifies as an "uitzendonderneming" and its workers as agency workers, reversing the 2024 district court ruling. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2618890); [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/hof_amsterdam_merkt_temper_aan_als_uitzendonderneming.html); [Loyens & Loeff](https://www.loyensloeff.com/nl/insights/news--events/news/gerechtshof-amsterdam-temper-werkers-kwalificeren-als-uitzendkrachten/)
- The court also found that Temper wrongly charged workers EUR 1 per hour between 2016 and 2019 and must repay it. — [Rendement](https://www.rendement.nl/werken-met-zzp-ers/nieuws/werkenden-via-temper-zijn-toch-uitzendkrachten.html); [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/06/temper-gig-workers-could-be-owed-back-pay-after-ruling/)
- Temper has appealed in cassation to the Hoge Raad; the ruling was declared "niet uitvoerbaar bij voorraad", so Temper need not implement it pending that appeal. — [Temper newsroom](https://go.temper.works/en-nl/newsroom/temper-appeals-at-supreme-court-what-it-means)
- Temper says "tens of thousands" of people work through the platform. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2618890)
- FNV: everyone who ever worked via Temper can claim back pay (holiday pay, sick pay, pension). — [FNV](https://www.fnv.nl/nieuwsbericht/algemeen-nieuws/2026/06/uitspraak-gerechtshof-over-temper-is-klinkende-ove)
- The ruling concerns only Temper's way of working and is not a general judgment on self-employment. — [De Clercq](https://declercq.com/en/blogs/het-temper-arrest-platformwerkers-zijn-uitzendkrachten)

**YoungOnes**
- Example rate: EUR 16.20 for the worker plus EUR 4.75 for platform and insurance, EUR 20.95 per hour in total (one worked example, not an official rate card). — [Quasa](https://quasa.io/nl/media/youngones-of-temper-eur005-verschil-maar-het-hof-noemt-temper-uitzendwerk)
- FNV considers YoungOnes to use a comparable construction to Temper; YoungOnes denies this. The Temper ruling contains no judgment on YoungOnes. — [Quasa](https://quasa.io/nl/media/youngones-of-temper-eur005-verschil-maar-het-hof-noemt-temper-uitzendwerk)
- YoungOnes publicly supported the "Zelfstandigenwet" bill in the June 2025 internet consultation. — [Internetconsultatie](https://www.internetconsultatie.nl/zelfstandigenwet/reactie/282483/bestand)

**Helpling (defunct in NL)**
- Helpling Netherlands was declared bankrupt in Amsterdam in January 2023, seven years after starting in the Netherlands. — [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/news/2023/01/domestic-cleaning-agency-helpling-goes-bust-in-nl/); [Eurofound](https://apps.eurofound.europa.eu/platformeconomydb/helpling-netherlands-declared-bankrupt-by-court-in-amsterdam-110214)
- In April 2025 the Supreme Court ruled that cleaners who worked via the bankrupt Helpling must be considered temporary agency workers. — [act legal](https://actlegal.com/publications/uitspraak-helpling)

**Level.works**
- Described as a platform connecting companies with flexible workers in logistics, transport, delivery and security (Dealroom profile with an AI-generated summary, weak evidence). — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/level_works)

**Other**
- Au pair platform Nina.care went bankrupt about a year after losing its Dutch licence; the founders continue via sister company Juno.care (adjacent, not a ZZP task platform). — [MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/nina-care-failliet-maar-de-kok-zussen-geven-niet-op-hoe-realistisch-is-een-uber-voor-au-pairs)

**Regulatory context that changes these platforms' model**
- A legal presumption of employment tied to an hourly rate was adopted by the Tweede Kamer on 21 April 2026 and the Eerste Kamer on 16 June 2026; below EUR 38 per hour the burden of proof shifts to the client. The Wet DBA stays in force; only the clarification part of the Wet VBAR was withdrawn. — [Rabobank](https://www.rabobank.nl/bedrijven/eigen-bedrijf-starten/financien/uurtarief-berekenen-zzp-2025)
- Temper states the presumption applies from 31 December 2026. — [Temper newsroom](https://go.temper.works/en-gb/newsroom/legal-presumption-at-a-low-hourly-rate-what-does-it-mean-for-clients)
- In 2025 the Belastingdienst resumed checks on false self-employment with a transition period (warning first). — [Horecava](https://www.horecava.nl/en/news/zzp-regels-horeca-wat-verandert-er)
- Freelance work volumes fell sharply: "42 percent less work for freelancers than 2 years ago" (September 2026), and fewer freelancers in the jobs market as the tax office gets tough (February 2026). — [NL Times](https://nltimes.nl/2026/09/03/now-42-percent-less-work-freelancers-2-years-ago); [DutchNews](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/02/fewer-freelancers-in-dutch-jobs-market-as-tax-office-gets-tough/)

### Inferences
- Shift platforms pay workers roughly EUR 15.50-16.20 per hour, far below the EUR 38 presumption threshold, so from 31 December 2026 every such shift carries a presumed employment relationship unless the client can rebut it. Combined with the Temper ruling, the ZZP shift-platform model in its current form looks hard to sustain.
- I found no report that Temper has already changed its model after the June 2026 ruling; it is litigating instead.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of any shift-platform bankruptcy in 2024-2026 other than the older Helpling (2023) case; I could not confirm or rule out closures of Brisk, Level.works, Roamler or Charly Cares. No status information at all was found for Brisk, Roamler or Charly Cares.
- No user or revenue numbers for Temper (beyond "tens of thousands") or YoungOnes.
- FNV claims the Labour Inspectorate concluded in a report that YoungOnes is an employment agency; the report itself was not found, so this is a union claim only.
- Uber / Deliveroo-type delivery platforms: not researched in this session beyond a general NOS reference to the Uber ruling.

## 3. Professional freelance boards: pricing and model

### Takeaway
For higher-skilled freelancers the market splits into open job boards (Freelance.nl, Hoofdkraan, Jellow), marketplace platforms with client-side fees (Malt) and broker/MSP intermediaries (HeadFirst/Striive, Magnit, Between, Circle8, Yacht, Hays) that sit between freelancer and large client. Verified pricing is thin: only Freelance.nl PRO (EUR 99/year, 2019 figure), Malt (10% client fee, no freelancer fee in NL) and Jellow (free for freelancers, subscription for companies) were sourced.

### Cited Findings
- **Freelance.nl**: free basic account with a premium option. — [ZZP Nederland comparison](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/waar-vind-je-als-zzper-opdrachten-vergelijk-de-beste-marktplaatsen)
- Freelance.nl PRO was EUR 99 per year excl. VAT in a 2019 support post; PRO gives unlimited responses and no waiting time; average assignment on the platform runs 6-9 months. Price may be outdated. — [Freelance.nl community](https://www.freelance.nl/community/het-freelancer-pro-account); [Freelance PRO page](https://www.freelance.nl/voor-freelancers/freelance-pro)
- Freelance.nl publishes the quarterly Freelance Markt Index (FMI), using CBS and ABU data plus a survey of at least 125 clients. Index: 95 in Q1 2026, 99 in Q2 2026, 89 in Q3 2026 (100 = growth benchmark); assignments on the platform rose 2% in Q1 2026 quarter on quarter. — [FMI Q1 2026](https://lp.freelance.nl/hubfs/Freelance%20Markt%20Index%20Q1%202026.pdf); [FMI Q2 2026](https://lp.freelance.nl/hubfs/Freelance%20Markt%20Index%20Q2%202026.pdf); [FMI Q3 2026](https://lp.freelance.nl/hubfs/Freelance%20Markt%20Index%20Q3%202026.pdf)
- FMI Q2 2026: freelancers ask on average 22% more than clients offer; "more ZZP'ers, less work per ZZP'er". — [Freelance.nl](https://www.freelance.nl/community/freelancers-vragen-22-meer-dan-opdrachtgevers-bieden)
- **Hoofdkraan.nl**: business-oriented, connects freelancers with companies; described by a third party as the go-to platform for creative freelancers. — [ZZP Nederland comparison](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/waar-vind-je-als-zzper-opdrachten-vergelijk-de-beste-marktplaatsen); [Striive blog](https://striive.com/en/blog/best-freelance-platforms-netherlands)
- **Jellow**: free for freelancers; companies pay a monthly subscription instead of a margin per assignment; positioned against "expensive intermediaries" and promotes direct contracting (partnership with ZZP Nederland dates from 2016). — [MT/Sprout Challenger50](https://mtsprout.nl/challenger50/challenger50-jellow-rekent-af-met-dure-intermediairs); [ZZP Nederland](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/nieuws/zzp-nederland-en-jellow-helpen-zzpers-met-directe-contractering)
- **Malt (NL)**: clients pay a service fee on top of the freelancer's rate, 10% on the Starter offer, varying by plan (Starter, Advanced, Enterprise). — [Malt help centre](https://help.malt.com/hc/en-150/articles/29567589850002-Malt-service-offers-and-fees)
- Malt freelancer-side fee, conflicting sources: an older blog says 10% for the first three months with a client, then 5%; a 2026 source citing Malt's regional fee page says freelancers based in the Netherlands are not charged any freelancer-side fee. — [Accountable](https://www.accountable.eu/nl-be/blog/freelancer-platform-malt/); contradicted by [Jobbers.io](https://www.jobbers.io/?p=160335); see also [Malt help centre](https://help.malt.com/hc/en-150/articles/29539691425938-How-does-the-Malt-commission-work-for-freelancers)
- **HeadFirst Group / Striive**: both the client and the independent professional pay for the services they use; the professional chooses a Basic, Premium or Excellent service package. No percentages in the source. — [HeadFirst service desk](https://servicedesk.headfirst.nl/hc/en-gb/articles/7026783719185-What-do-HeadFirst-s-services-cost)
- HeadFirst Group revenue reported as up 38% to over EUR 2 billion (snippet undated and possibly mixing years; treat as unverified for 2025). — [Consultancy.nl](https://www.consultancy.nl/nieuws/63266/acht-vragen-en-antwoorden-over-de-12-miljoen-zzpers-in-nederland)
- **Planet Interim**: publishes the Planet Interim Index; interim IT assignments fell for the first time in 2024 while the supply of interim IT professionals rose 66%. — [ZiPconomy](https://www.zipconomy.nl/tag/interim-2/)
- **General job boards used by ZZP'ers**: Nationale Vacaturebank (free; construction, technical, government work), Jobbird (free, broad ZZP vacancies), Indeed and ViaPlaats (local/regional jobs; ViaPlaats free, nearly all sectors). — [ZZP Nederland comparison](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/waar-vind-je-als-zzper-opdrachten-vergelijk-de-beste-marktplaatsen)
- ZZP Nederland published a position paper (September 2026) and an article arguing that the self-employed "pay a high price for the false security of intermediaries". — [ZZP Nederland](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/nieuws/zelfstandigen-betalen-hoge-prijs-voor-schijnveiligheid-intermediairs)
- ZZP Nederland's survey data: many new jobs come through fellow entrepreneurs, the informal network and social media such as LinkedIn; it advises using several platforms at once. — [ZZP Nederland comparison](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/waar-vind-je-als-zzper-opdrachten-vergelijk-de-beste-marktplaatsen)

### Inferences
- These boards are aimed at knowledge workers (IT, interim, creative, consultancy). Freelance.nl lists construction roles such as "bouwmanager", but none of them is a channel for tradesmen's hands-on jobs.
- Wet DBA enforcement has pushed large clients toward intermediaries that take over compliance, which is the practice ZZP Nederland criticises as costly for the freelancer.

### Gaps
- No pricing found for Hoofdkraan (premium subscription), Freep, Planet Interim, IT-contracts, Freelancer.nl, or the exact fee percentages of HeadFirst/Striive, Magnit (ex-Brainnet), Between, Circle8, Yacht and Hays.
- No current Jellow subscription price for companies; no current Freelance.nl PRO price (only the 2019 EUR 99/year).
- No user counts or assignment counts for Freelance.nl, Hoofdkraan, Jellow, Malt NL. No ZiPconomy ranking of intermediaries by revenue was retrieved.
- LinkedIn and Indeed pricing for freelancers was not researched.

## 4. Construction / technical subcontracting-specific platforms for ZZP

### Takeaway
I found no established, large Dutch marketplace dedicated to matching ZZP tradesmen with main contractors for subcontracting; official guidance points construction ZZP'ers to the consumer lead platforms, general job boards and their own network. The only dedicated entrants found are small or new: BUKKI (June 2025, aimed at foreign-language, mainly Polish, entrepreneurs), WorkForMe and the tender-oriented 12Build.

### Cited Findings
- **BUKKI** (eFaktura.nl): launched 16 June 2025 in Amsterdam; connects entrepreneurs in construction, installation and transport directly to assignments and tradesmen for joint projects; described as a search engine for entrepreneurs in the Netherlands, Belgium and Germany, with nine languages and automatic translation of assignments. — [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/wire/efakturanl-lanceert-bukki-opdrachtenplatform-anderstalige-ondernemers-bouw-techniek)
- BUKKI access is exclusive to registered eFaktura.nl users; eFaktura.nl says it is used daily by more than 20,000 companies and was originally built to support entrepreneurs mainly from the Polish community with administration. — [Emerce](https://www.emerce.nl/wire/efakturanl-lanceert-bukki-opdrachtenplatform-anderstalige-ondernemers-bouw-techniek); [NPCC member profile](https://npcc.pl/members/efaktura-nl/)
- **WorkForMe**: an online platform for contractors looking for a permanent or replacement subcontractor (Belgian source, date unconfirmed). — [Made in Kempen](https://www.made-in.be/kempen/nieuw-platform-brengt-aannemers-en-onderaannemers-samen/)
- **12Build**: an existing construction tendering platform; subcontractors get document navigation, automatic notifications of new projects and a "Bid board". It handles quotes and project documents, not the matching of individuals. — [Generation-NT press release](https://www.generation-nt.com/communiques-presse/much-more-than-promotion-in-construction-12build-supports-2082303)
- Government and KvK guidance for construction ZZP'ers covers finding jobs and the subcontracting rules (VAT reverse charge to the main contractor, chain liability, G-rekening) rather than naming a dedicated subcontracting marketplace. — [Ondernemersplein](https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/ondernemen-in-de-bouw-zo-vindt-u-opdrachten/); [KvK](https://www.kvk.nl/starten/starten-als-zzper-in-de-bouw-dit-moet-je-regelen/)
- ZZP Nederland's comparison names Werkspot, Homedeal, Nationale Vacaturebank, Jobbird, Indeed and ViaPlaats as the channels for construction and technical work. — [ZZP Nederland comparison](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/waar-vind-je-als-zzper-opdrachten-vergelijk-de-beste-marktplaatsen)

### Inferences
- The B2B side of construction ZZP work (ZZP'er hired by a contractor) appears to run through intermediaries, staffing-style agencies, personal networks and social media rather than through an open marketplace. That is a visible hole in the landscape, and BUKKI is the only product found that targets it directly.
- BUKKI's reach is capped by design at eFaktura.nl's user base, so it is a feature of an invoicing tool, not an open market.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on Bouwjobs, "ZZP Bouw" platforms, Technicum-type intermediaries or Werkzoeken as ZZP channels: no pricing, ownership or scale.
- TenderNed and Negometrix/Mercell (public tenders) were not researched; their relevance for small jobs is unverified.
- BUKKI's pricing, number of assignments and active users are not published in the sources found.

## 5. Informal channels: Facebook groups, WhatsApp groups, Marktplaats, Polish portals

### Takeaway
Informal channels clearly carry a large share of jobs, but their size could not be measured: no verified member counts for any Dutch or Polish Facebook group and nothing on niedziela.nl or WhatsApp groups was retrievable. The evidence is indirect: Polish job boards carry Dutch construction work offered on a ZZP/B2B basis, and survey data say network and social media are leading sources of new work.

## 6. Which platforms are the largest? Is there a dominant one-stop marketplace? New AI-based entrants 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
There is no one-stop marketplace: the market is segmented by job type, with Werkspot dominant only in consumer home-services leads, shift platforms (Temper, YoungOnes) in low-wage gigs, and brokers such as HeadFirst Group in professional contracting. No AI-based job-matching entrant for ZZP tradesmen was found in 2025-2026; the AI products found address other tasks (measurement, tender documents).

### Cited Findings
- Werkspot calls itself market leader in the Netherlands with more than 350,000 jobs per year, and reports 45,000 hiring clients per month in 2025. — [Werkspot over-ons](https://www.werkspot.nl/over-ons); [Werkspot kwaliteitscontrole](https://www.werkspot.nl/kwaliteitscontrole)

### Inferences
- "Largest by number of jobs" is only answerable for Werkspot (350,000+ jobs a year). No other platform publishes a comparable count, so a ranking by jobs or users cannot be built from public sources.
- The absence of a one-stop marketplace is structural: consumer leads, hourly gigs and B2B contracting have different payers, different legal regimes and different pricing, and no player spans them.
- The open segment is B2B construction subcontracting for small ZZP tradesmen, including non-Dutch speakers. BUKKI's launch and the paid effort to build Polish ZZP Facebook groups both point at that unmet demand.

### Gaps
- No traffic data (Similarweb), app-store data, Crunchbase/Dealroom funding data or KvK filings were retrieved, so scale comparisons rest on the platforms' own claims.
- I searched once for AI-based matching entrants and found none; that is weak evidence of absence, not proof. Cobouw coverage was not reachable.
- Rows marked n/f for owner are known in the trade but were not sourced here; they should be filled from KvK or the platforms' "over ons" pages once those hosts are reachable.
