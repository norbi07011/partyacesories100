# Documented flaws, complaints and failures of Dutch ZZP / tradesman job marketplaces (2023-2026)

> **Method note for the report writer (read first).** Research date: 28 Nov 2026. In this session direct page fetches were blocked for trustpilot.com, radar-forum.avrotros.nl, rechtspraak.nl and zzp-nederland.nl (hosts outside the session's network allow-list). All findings below therefore come from **search-engine result summaries of the cited pages**, not from my own reading of the full pages. Quotes are as rendered by the search tool (several were translated to English by it; the Dutch originals were not visible). Treat every quote as "paraphrase-level accurate" and re-verify verbatim wording before publishing it in quotation marks. Each bullet is tagged **[VERIFIED FACT]** (court, regulator, official statistic, named survey, company's own terms) or **[ANECDOTAL]** (self-selected reviews, forum posts, competitor blogs).

## 1. Lead platforms (Werkspot / Homedeal / Solvari / Zoofy / Offerte.nl): what tradesmen and consumers complain about

### Takeaway
The core, structurally unavoidable complaint is the pay-per-lead model: tradesmen pay EUR 3-96 for contact details, the same request is sold to several competitors, and nothing is refunded when the customer never answers; consumers in turn get no response on small jobs, variable quality and a platform that contractually disclaims responsibility. Headline ratings are nonetheless decent (Werkspot ~3.9/5 on Trustpilot), so the dissatisfaction is concentrated on the supply (tradesman) side and on small jobs. I found **no ACM enforcement action, no Kassa/Radar TV investigation and no Consumentenbond comparative test** specifically targeting these platforms in 2023-2026.

### Cited Findings

**Werkspot - facts**
- [VERIFIED FACT] Trustpilot: TrustScore 3.9 ("Great"), roughly 7,400-8,000 reviews (count differs between Trustpilot country mirrors/time of crawl). — [Trustpilot Werkspot](https://nz.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl)
- [VERIFIED FACT - company's own statement] Werkspot charges pros a lead fee to obtain the customer's name, e-mail and phone number; in a Trustpilot reply Werkspot states the fee "can vary from EUR 3.00 to EUR 96.00" depending partly on job size. Registration is free, no subscription. — [Werkspot FAQ](https://www.werkspot.nl/blog/faq); [Trustpilot Werkspot p.5](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl?page=5)
- [VERIFIED FACT - company's own terms] Werkspot's general terms say it has only a facilitating role and bears no responsibility for how users fulfil their obligations (i.e. no guarantee on the work). — [Werkspot algemene voorwaarden](https://www.werkspot.nl/algemene-voorwaarden)
- [VERIFIED FACT - company's own statement] Werkspot says that if a lead turns out to be false, no costs are charged (refund on request, burden on the pro). — [Trustpilot Werkspot p.10](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl?page=10)

**Werkspot - tradesman complaints (anecdotal)**
- [ANECDOTAL] Lead cost vs job size: "for some jobs lasting an hour to two hours, they charge EUR 12 to EUR 24 or more in lead costs". — [Trustpilot Werkspot p.5](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl?page=5)
- [ANECDOTAL] Paying for non-converting leads: "customers hardly respond, while you still have to buy paid leads. As a result, you don't earn back your costs"; a reviewer adds that this scares tradespeople off and leaves customers with a job that gets no response. — [Trustpilot Werkspot p.10](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl?page=10)
- [ANECDOTAL] Fake leads: a pro says he paid EUR 50 to talk to what turned out to be a scammer with no work; a 2025 review complains of many fake or non-responsive contacts. A February 2026 reviewer calls the company "oplichters" (scammers); a one-star pro review says contact-detail costs are too high. — [Trustpilot Werkspot](https://nz.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl); [Trustpilot p.8](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl?page=8)
- [ANECDOTAL] Multiple contractors pay for the same job: Radar forum users note that when a consumer replies to three handymen, three tradesmen each pay for one job; thread title "Werkspot waardeloos voor vakmannen" ("Werkspot worthless for tradesmen"). Threads are archived and more than three years old. — [Radar forum thread](https://radar-forum.avrotros.nl/overig-huis-tuin-f90/werkspot-waardeloos-voor-vakmannen-t186841.html); [Radar forum post](https://radar-forum.avrotros.nl/viewtopic.php?p=2696860)
- [ANECDOTAL - source is a marketing agency selling an alternative, treat as estimate] "One request is usually sold to three to five tradespeople at the same time"; leads cost EUR 25-50 and pros win roughly one in four or five, implying EUR 100-250 acquisition cost per won job. — [Veentjer Agency blog](https://veentjeragency.com/blog/is-werkspot-het-waard)
- [ANECDOTAL] One reviewer claims on-platform Werkspot reviews are mostly fake (unverified allegation); a consistent theme is that Werkspot works for larger jobs while small jobs are not worth the lead fee. — [Feedback Company Werkspot](https://www.feedbackcompany.com/nl-nl/reviews/werkspot-nl/)
- [ANECDOTAL - positive counter-evidence] A consumer reports a painter saying he gets a lot of work via Werkspot and benefits from its reviews. — [Trustpilot Werkspot p.5](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/www.werkspot.nl?page=5)

**Solvari**
- [VERIFIED FACT] Trustpilot lists ~3,766 reviews for solvari.nl and ~2,365 for solvari.be (score not captured). — [Trustpilot Solvari NL](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.solvari.nl); [Trustpilot Solvari BE](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/solvari.be)
- [ANECDOTAL] Consumers: "they have all non professional companies and only companies which pay solvari"; "a paid company ghosted me". — [Trustpilot Solvari NL](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/www.solvari.nl)
- [ANECDOTAL] Public complaints on Test-Aankoop (Belgian consumer body's complaint board), 2026: a Dutch contractor writes "Echte vakmannen noemen jullie oplichters" ("real tradesmen call you scammers"); a business says it is being pursued for about EUR 3,000 under an old Offrea contract it says it never signed with Solvari (contract lock-in / inherited contracts after acquisition). — [Test-Aankoop Solvari page](https://www.test-aankoop.be/klagen/bedrijven/solvari/36F7B029-B713DF550); [Test-Aankoop complaint "van Offrea naar Solvari"](https://www.test-aankoop.be/klagen/publieke-klachten/van-offrea-naar-solvari/CPTBE01821069-88)

**Zoofy (fixed-price booking model)**
- [VERIFIED FACT] ~4,030 Trustpilot reviews; Zoofy sells an optional warranty on completed work for EUR 6.95 per job (i.e. guarantee is an upsell, not default). — [Trustpilot Zoofy](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zoofy.nl?page=7); [Zoofy blog](https://zoofy.nl/en/blog/zoofy-reviews-complaints-and-reliability)
- [ANECDOTAL] Consumers: a handyman said "his standard rate when hired directly is about 20-25% less than the one from Zoofy"; Zoofy "charges an extra 15% for any materials used"; "no direct support line - only a chatbot with a 48-hour email response time". Zoofy's reply: it "needs to make some money to continue providing this service". — [Trustpilot Zoofy p.7](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zoofy.nl?page=7); [Trustpilot Zoofy p.9](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zoofy.nl?page=9)

**Consumer-side context**
- [VERIFIED FACT - but secondary citation] A 2024 Consumentenbond survey reportedly found 72% of klus-platform users had a positive experience, with a warning about service fees on top of hourly rates (cited via a third-party magazine; primary not seen). — [Expert-zoom](https://expert-zoom.com/nl/tijdschrift/huisverbetering/klusjesman-gezocht)
- [VERIFIED FACT] Consumentenbond and Radar advise consumers to verify tradesmen outside the platform (trade-association membership, Klachtenkompas, Radar forum, Opgelicht?!), and note that only branch membership gives access to the Geschillencommissie. — [Consumentenbond](https://www.consumentenbond.nl/verbouwen-en-klussen/schilder-of-klusjesman-vinden); [Radar](https://radar.avrotros.nl/artikel/zo-vind-je-een-betrouwbare-klusjesman-30968)
- [VERIFIED FACT - company-commissioned] Werkspot reports 75% of consumers who outsourced a job checked the tradesman's reliability beforehand. — [Werkspot blog](https://www.werkspot.nl/blog/driekwart-nederlanders-checkt-vakman-vooraf-op-betrouwbaarheid)

### Inferences
- The lead model misaligns incentives: the platform earns on contact-sharing, not on completed jobs, so it profits from selling one request several times and from low-intent requests. That is the same mechanic the US FTC sanctioned at HomeAdvisor (see section 6).
- Small jobs are structurally under-served: a EUR 12-24 lead fee at a ~20-25% win rate makes a 1-2 hour job unprofitable, so tradesmen stop responding, which is exactly the consumer complaint "no reaction".
- Consumer star ratings (3.9) hide supply-side resentment; the "wow" is absent because neither side gets certainty: the pro has no certainty of a job, the consumer none of quality or recourse.
- Zoofy shows the opposite trade-off: fixed prices and booking fix the lead problem but the margin (20-25% above direct price, +15% on materials) creates a strong incentive to go direct on the second job.
- That official consumer advice is "check the tradesman elsewhere" means platform verification is not trusted as sufficient.

### Gaps
- Homedeal and Offerte.nl: no specific evidence retrieved (no ratings, no complaints).
- No ACM decision, fine or investigation against Werkspot/Solvari/Homedeal/Zoofy found; no Kassa or Radar broadcast specifically about these platforms in 2023-2026 found. This may be a search limitation; ACM and Kassa sites were not searched directly.
- Star-distribution percentages, Klantenvertellen scores, Google Play/App Store ratings: not retrieved (Trustpilot fetch blocked).
- Reddit (r/thenetherlands, r/klussers), Tweakers, Bouwforum, Facebook groups: no threads surfaced in search; no evidence either way.
- Account-blocking and rising-lead-price complaints: not evidenced beyond generic "too expensive" reviews.
- Radar forum quotes are older than 2023 (archived threads).

## 2. Gig platforms (Temper / YoungOnes / Level.works): fees, rates, false self-employment, enforcement

### Takeaway
The decisive 2026 fact: on 16 June 2026 the Amsterdam Court of Appeal ruled that Temper workers are agency workers (uitzendkrachten), not self-employed, overturning the 2024 district-court win for Temper and opening the door to back-pay claims; Temper reportedly goes to the Supreme Court. Combined with the lifting of the DBA enforcement moratorium on 1 Jan 2025, the legal foundation of the hourly "zzp gig" model is now the platforms' biggest weakness, on top of long-standing complaints about low rates and hourly platform fees.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED FACT] Gerechtshof Amsterdam, 16 June 2026 (ECLI:NL:GHAMS:2026:1612, ECLI as reported by trade press): Temper workers are uitzendkrachten; the client determines hours and instructions, which fits the uitzendovereenkomst; Temper and its clients acted contrary to the WAADI; workers may be able to claim wages/allowances retroactively; unions may still bring collective claims. Ruling is limited to Temper's working method. — [Rechtspraak.nl press release](https://www.rechtspraak.nl/organisatie-en-contact/organisatie/gerechtshoven/gerechtshof-amsterdam/nieuws/2026/06/werkers-van-temper-zijn-uitzendkrachten); [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2618890); [Loyens & Loeff](https://www.loyensloeff.com/nl/insights/news--events/news/gerechtshof-amsterdam-temper-werkers-kwalificeren-als-uitzendkrachten/); [PwC](https://www.pwc.nl/nl/actueel-en-publicaties/belastingnieuws/loonbelasting-en-sociale-verzekeringen/hof_amsterdam_merkt_temper_aan_als_uitzendonderneming.html)
- [VERIFIED FACT] FNV calls it a "klinkende overwinning" (resounding victory) for FNV and CNV. — [FNV](https://www.fnv.nl/nieuwsbericht/algemeen-nieuws/2026/06/uitspraak-gerechtshof-over-temper-is-klinkende-ove)
- [VERIFIED FACT] First instance (Rechtbank Amsterdam, 2024): Temper workers were not agency workers; no formal employer authority, clients pay the workers. Unions called it "een onbegrijpelijke uitspraak" and appealed. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2528289)
- [CONFLICTING] Cassation: one source says Temper decided to appeal to the Hoge Raad; an earlier SRA item says it was still considering. Not independently confirmed. — [ZZP Nederland](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/nieuws/hof-werkers-van-temper-zijn-uitzendkrachten-en-geen-zelfstandig-ondernemers); [SRA](https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/004501/2026/06/gerechtshof-temper-is-een-uitzendbureau)
- [VERIFIED FACT] FNV states YoungOnes and other platforms use a comparable construction; YoungOnes disputes being an agency. — [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2528289)
- [VERIFIED FACT] Since 1 Jan 2025 the Belastingdienst again enforces against schijnzelfstandigheid (moratorium lifted). KHN (hospitality trade body) warns that the client bears most of the fiscal and labour-law risk. Sources differ on intensity: KHN's 2026 headline is that the Belastingdienst "handhaaft nog niet volledig in 2026". — [KHN](https://khn.nl/nieuws/zzp-en-schijnzelfstandigheid-belastingdienst-handhaaft-nog-niet-volledig-in-2026); [KHN Q&A](https://khn.nl/veelgestelde-vragen/q-a-over-zzpers-en-schijnzelfstandigheid)
- [VERIFIED FACT - employer survey] AWVN member survey Feb 2025 (242 employers): half stop or reduce hiring ZZP'ers; 43% try to convert them to employment, only 8% say freelancers are willing; over a quarter report higher labour costs from switching flex form. 2026 follow-up (162 respondents): 47% offered ZZP'ers a contract, 6% say they actually accept. — [AWVN 2025](https://www.awvn.nl/platformeconomie-zzpers/publicaties/ledenonderzoek-2025-helft-werkgevers-stopt-of-vermindert-inhuur-zzpers/); [AWVN 2026](https://www.awvn.nl/platformeconomie-zzpers/publicaties/ledenonderzoek-2026-flexwerk/)
- [VERIFIED FACT] CBS: more than 4 in 10 companies expected to hire fewer ZZP'ers in 2025. — [CBS](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/nieuws/2025/10/ruim-4-op-de-10-bedrijven-verwachten-minder-zzp-ers-in-te-huren-in-2025)
- [VERIFIED FACT - company's own pages; figures inconsistent across pages] Temper charges clients a usage fee per worked hour: EUR 4.40 (help centre), EUR 3.90 (B2B page), EUR 4.90 flat for telemarketing. Factoring for fast payout is paid by the freelancer (Finqle, 2.9%). — [Temper help](https://help.temper.works/en/articles/4592679-for-clients-this-is-how-temper-works); [Temper B2B](https://go.temper.works/nl-nl/download/b2b); [Temper client FAQ](https://help.temper.works/en/articles/6660579-frequently-asked-questions-by-clients)
- [ANECDOTAL - older, pre-2023] MT/Sprout: former "FreeFlexers" criticise low rates ("13 euro per uur, daar werk ik niet voor") and the effort needed to collect unpaid invoices. — [MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/ontevreden-oud-freeflexers-over-temper-13-euro-per-uur-daar-werk-ik-niet-voor)
- [ANECDOTAL] Indeed reviewer: Temper leaves the freelancer to fend for himself when problems arise while earning on each of his hours; other Indeed reviews praise the app as effective and clear. — [Indeed Temper](https://nl.indeed.com/cmp/Temper/reviews)
- [VERIFIED FACT - union claim, 2020] FNV "witboek" claimed society loses EUR 18 million through Temper's model (unpaid premiums/taxes). Advocacy figure, not adjudicated. — [FNV witboek](https://www.fnv.nl/getattachment/Nieuwsbericht/Algemeen-nieuws/2020/06/Maatschappij-loopt-18-miljoen-euro-mis-door-Temper/FNV-witboek-Temper-2.pdf?lang=nl-NL); [MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/vakbonden-kraken-temper-maatschappij-loopt-18-miljoen-mis)
- [VERIFIED FACT - historical] Arbeidsinspectie earlier took the position that Temper freelancers are not independent entrepreneurs. — [MT/Sprout](https://mtsprout.nl/groei/arbeidsinspecteur-houdt-poot-stijf-temper-freelancers-zijn-geen-zelfstandig-ondernemer)

### Inferences
- Hourly-shift gig platforms in NL carry existential regulatory risk; any new marketplace built on "zzp per hour under client instruction" inherits it. Project/result-based work for consumers (a tradesman doing a defined job) is legally far safer than shift work for businesses.
- The fee stack (EUR 3.90-4.90/hour to the client plus 2.9% factoring from the worker) on low-wage shifts is a visible wedge that pushes both sides to deal directly once they know each other.
- The employer surveys suggest demand-side retreat from ZZP hiring in general after 2025, which shrinks platform liquidity regardless of product quality.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of specific clients leaving Temper/YoungOnes after 2025, nor platform revenue/volume figures.
- No data on no-show rates, rating-system complaints, or app-store ratings for Temper/YoungOnes.
- Level.works: nothing retrieved.
- No Belastingdienst action against a named platform found.
- The Temper rate/fee complaints found are mostly pre-2023.

## 3. Freelance job boards and broker chains (Freelance.nl / Hoofdkraan / Planet Interim / Striive)

### Takeaway
Evidence here is thinner and mostly anecdotal: the documented irritants are paywalls to respond (Freelance.nl PRO), silent subscription renewal, and listings posted by recruiters/intermediaries rather than end clients. The hardest fact on broker chains is the 2019 TCP/ING case (hundreds of freelancers unpaid through a broker), with an estimated 250,000 ZZP'ers dependent on intermediaries.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED FACT - company's own pages] Freelance.nl: free account gives access to a limited number of jobs; Freelance PRO unlocks direct access to all jobs. Listings come from both organisations and intermediaries. Historic price EUR 99/year ex VAT (2019, outdated); a Trustpilot company reply values a PRO+ quarterly subscription at EUR 150. — [Freelance.nl PRO](https://www.freelance.nl/voor-freelancers/freelance-pro); [Freelance.nl community](https://www.freelance.nl/community/het-freelancer-pro-account); [Trustpilot Freelance.nl](https://nl.trustpilot.com/review/freelance.nl?page=5)
- [ANECDOTAL] Trustpilot (Freelance.nl, ~163 reviews; scores seen between ~3.6 and 4.2 depending on page/time): complaints about tacit contract renewal ("the worst is how they silently extend your contract"), accidental PRO purchase with poor support, businesses held to a 12-month term, and that vacancies are often from recruiters so you learn nothing about the assignment. **Caution: search results mixed Freelance.nl with the unrelated Freelancer.nl (~351 reviews); unsolicited-upgrade/invoice complaints may belong to Freelancer.nl.** — [Trustpilot Freelance.nl](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/freelance.nl?page=4); [Trustpilot Freelancer.nl](https://nl.trustpilot.com/review/freelancer.nl?page=5)
- [VERIFIED FACT] 2019 TCP/ING: hundreds of freelancers working at ING were not paid for months due to cash-flow problems at broker TCP (one source: 850 complainants); ING declined responsibility because TCP was formally the client. PZO and Stichting ZZP Nederland warned of proliferation of "flexmakelaars". — [Accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2019/8/zorgen-zzp-organisaties-om-wildgroei-flexmakelaars/); [Accountancy Vanmorgen](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2019/08/26/zorgen-in-zzp-markt-over-flexbemiddelaars/)
- [VERIFIED FACT - survey claim] About 250,000 ZZP'ers depend partly or wholly on intermediaries (ZZP Barometer figure as reported). — [Accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2019/8/zorgen-zzp-organisaties-om-wildgroei-flexmakelaars/)
- [VERIFIED FACT - advice from ZZP body] ZZP Nederland tells freelancers to watch intermediary margins and non-compete clauses that prevent dealing directly with the client next time. — [ZZP Nederland kennisbank](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/werken-als-zzper-een-intermediair-alles-wat-je-moet-weten)
- [VERIFIED FACT] ZZP Nederland published a position paper (Sept 2026) on working via intermediaries, calling for freedom of choice, transparency and protection against chain risk (content not read). — [ZZP Nederland position paper](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/sites/default/files/2026-09/Position%20paper%20ZZP%20Nederland%20intermediairs%20-def%20.pdf)
- [OPINION] ZiPconomy commentators attribute the problem to opaque practices of intermediaries, including "doorleenconstructies" (sub-lending chains). — [Accountancy Vanmorgen](https://www.accountancyvanmorgen.nl/2019/08/26/zorgen-in-zzp-markt-over-flexbemiddelaars/)

### Inferences
- Charging the supply side to respond (PRO) while much of the inventory is recruiter-posted creates the "pay to enter a lottery" feeling; this is the white-collar twin of the Werkspot lead fee.
- Non-compete clauses show that intermediaries know disintermediation is the natural outcome and fight it contractually rather than by adding value.

### Gaps
- No evidence retrieved on: ghost/expired listings, "hundreds of responses per assignment", actual margin percentages, typical payment terms (30/60/90 days), recruiter spam. These remain unsubstantiated assumptions.
- Hoofdkraan, Planet Interim, Striive: no complaint data or ratings retrieved (only that Hoofdkraan published an average rate of EUR 47/hour in 2019).
- Position-paper contents not read (host blocked).

## 4. Marktplaats services, Facebook and WhatsApp groups

### Takeaway
Informal channels have zero verification and are where rogue-tradesman fraud concentrates from the consumer's viewpoint; official advice is essentially "never prepay, verify identity and address yourself". I found no data on tradesmen being exploited or unpaid via these channels.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED FACT] Fraudehelpdesk reports an increase in reports of fraud by rogue tradesmen (fake locksmiths, electricians, urgent-repair scams); Kassa headline: "Vaker oplichting door malafide klussers". — [Kassa (BNNVARA)](https://www.bnnvara.nl/kassa/artikelen/vaker-oplichting-door-malafide-klussers-zo-trap-je-er-niet-in); [Hart van Nederland, Apr 2026](https://www.hartvannederland.nl/112/crime/artikelen/klusjesman-fraude-spoed-vakman-oplichting-tips-fraudehelpdesk)
- [VERIFIED FACT - advice] Never pay large amounts fully upfront; pay the bulk after satisfactory completion (Apr 2026). Rogue handymen often use a fake name with no visiting address, making recovery hard. — [Welingelichte Kringen](https://www.welingelichtekringen.nl/samenleving/waarschuwing-haal-geen-klusjesmannen-waar-je-niks-van-weet); [ID.nl](https://id.nl/huis-en-entertainment/huishouden/tuin-klussen/hoe-vind-je-een-echte-vakman)
- [VERIFIED FACT] Marktplaats warns users to stay inside Marktplaats messages and not be lured to WhatsApp/e-mail; payment-request fraud fell 77% over two years after measures (older article, Limburg police figures). — [Marktplaats](https://www.marktplaats.nl/m/betaalfraude/); [NOS](https://nos.nl/l/2462294)

### Inferences
- Demand for a trusted identity layer (KvK check, real address, insurance) and for milestone/escrow payment exists precisely because public advice tells consumers to build that protection themselves.

### Gaps
- No evidence on undeclared work, exploitation of (migrant) workers, or non-payment of tradesmen via Facebook/WhatsApp groups; no figures on the size of these groups.
- No Marktplaats "Diensten & Vakmensen"-specific complaint data.

## 5. Cross-cutting structural problems

### Takeaway
Late payment is the best-documented cross-cutting pain (48% of ZZP'ers paid late in the past 12 months; 23% wrote off revenue), and none of the lead or job-board platforms reviewed offers payment protection as a default. Other structural problems (fragmentation, duplicates, language, mobile UX) are plausible but I found no measured evidence.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED FACT - bank survey] Knab, Oct 2025 (600+ self-employed): 48% had clients paying invoices late in the past 12 months (unchanged since 2023); 19% sometimes have liquidity problems because of it; large companies pay late most often. — [Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/pers/onderzoek-helft-van-de-zzpers-krijgt-te-maken-met-te-late-betalingen); [Accountant.nl](https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2025/10/helft-van-zzpers-krijgt-te-laat-betaald-betaallinks-en-qr-codes-helpen/); [NL Times](https://nltimes.nl/2025/10/26/nearly-fifth-freelancers-face-financial-problems-due-late-payments)
- [VERIFIED FACT - bank survey] Knab: 23% of ZZP'ers wrote off revenue as uncollectable in the past year, on average 1.4% of annual turnover. — [Knab](https://bieb.knab.nl/ondernemen/onderzoek-helft-van-de-zzpers-krijgt-te-maken-met-te-late-betalingen)
- [VERIFIED FACT] Platform dependency is real for a minority: of the ~6% of ZZP'ers who acquire work via digital platforms, 30% depend on platforms for 60-100% of turnover. — [CBS Monitor online platformen 2022](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/rapportages/2023/monitor-online-platformen-2022/4-werkgelegenheid)
- [VERIFIED FACT - company terms] No guarantee/escrow by default: Werkspot disclaims responsibility for performance; Zoofy sells warranty as a EUR 6.95 add-on. — [Werkspot terms](https://www.werkspot.nl/algemene-voorwaarden); [Zoofy blog](https://zoofy.nl/en/blog/zoofy-reviews-complaints-and-reliability)
- [ANECDOTAL] Disintermediation incentive visible in reviews: tradesman's direct rate 20-25% below Zoofy's price. — [Trustpilot Zoofy](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/zoofy.nl?page=7)

### Inferences
- The three verified gaps a new entrant could attack are: (a) payment certainty for the worker, (b) outcome recourse for the client, (c) a pricing model that charges on completed work rather than on contact.
- Because platforms monetise the introduction only, they have little to offer on the second job, so repeat use happens off-platform by design.

### Gaps
- No construction-specific late-payment statistic found (Bill Incasso's 2026 "Nationaal onderzoek betalingsachterstanden MKB" covers construction and ZZP separately but figures were not visible: [PDF](https://billincasso.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Nationaal-onderzoek-betalingsachterstanden-MKB.pdf)).
- No evidence found for: number of competing sites/fragmentation, duplicated listings, language barriers for Polish/Eastern European tradesmen, mobile UX quality, price transparency studies, cold-start in NL.

## 6. Why such marketplaces fail to grow virally (international lessons)

### Takeaway
Analyses of Homejoy and the FTC's HomeAdvisor case point to the same structural causes visible in the Dutch complaints: trust-heavy recurring relationships leak off-platform, platform take-rates make direct dealing cheaper for both sides, growth bought with discounts does not retain, and lead-selling models are tempted to oversell lead quality.

### Cited Findings
- [ANALYSIS] Homejoy: "platform leakage" - a client finds a quality pro and takes them offline; home services are "a high-touch, trust-heavy model, with a recurring type of client-pro relationship" and so more leak-prone than rides or delivery. — [Harvard Digital Initiative](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/submission/homejoy-a-silicon-valley-darlings-path-to-the-grave/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/07/31/why-homejoy-failed-and-the-future-of-the-on-demand-economy)
- [ANALYSIS] Homejoy took a 25% cut; wage economics for pros "did not make sense", and preserving the margin meant above-market prices for market-quality cleaning. 75% of bookings came from discounts rather than referrals or organic growth, and customers rarely returned. — [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/article/248896); [Harvard Digital Initiative](https://d3.harvard.edu/platform-digit/submission/homejoys-not-so-joyous-demise/index.html)
- [VERIFIED FACT - founder's statement] Homejoy's co-founder named worker-classification lawsuits as the "deciding factor" in its shutdown (parallel to Temper 2026). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/07/31/why-homejoy-failed-and-the-future-of-the-on-demand-economy)
- [ANALYSIS] Durable service marketplaces either make the worker fungible (delivery) or become the system of record after the match (payroll, scheduling, compliance, invoicing, dispute handling, non-portable reputation). — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/chat/h/829c3e65-8244-4aa1-8334-44c2d0ca8c63/)
- [VERIFIED FACT - regulator] FTC v. HomeAdvisor (complaint Mar 2022, final order Jan 2023): alleged unsubstantiated claims about lead quality and source since mid-2014 and promoting a conversion rate higher than its own data supported; up to USD 7.2 million redress to contractors; ban on misleading lead claims. Settlement without admission; HomeAdvisor says it "was not found to have acted illegally". — [FTC case page](https://search.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/1923106-homeadvisor-matter); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/01/23/the-ftc-fines-homeadvisor-up-to-7-2m-for-lying-about-lead-quality-and-other-matters)

### Inferences
- Virality requires that a satisfied user has a reason to bring the next transaction and other people onto the platform. In lead models the happiest outcome (client finds a great tradesman) ends platform usage; the tradesman gains nothing by inviting peers (they are competitors for the same lead), and the consumer need is infrequent. There is therefore no built-in referral loop on either side.
- The NL pattern (Werkspot lead complaints, Zoofy markup, Temper classification ruling) maps one-to-one onto the Homejoy/HomeAdvisor failure modes: leakage, take-rate, lead quality, labour law.
- A plausible design conclusion: monetise post-match value (payment guarantee, invoicing, warranty, dispute resolution, portable verified reputation) rather than the introduction.

### Gaps
- Nothing retrieved on Thumbtack, Angi (beyond HomeAdvisor), Checkatrade or MyHammer; no NL-specific marketplace-economics analysis found.
- Homejoy sources are 2015-era student/analyst pieces, not peer-reviewed.

## 7. Evidence of unmet need: how ZZP'ers actually get work

### Takeaway
Across every survey found, network, repeat customers and word of mouth dominate; platforms are a channel for only about 5-6% of ZZP'ers in official statistics (higher, ~23-42%, in self-selected surveys that lump intermediaries and marketplaces together). Most figures are old (2017-2022), so current channel shares are a real data gap.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED FACT - dated 2017, business-services ZZP only] KvK: formal network (83%) and previous satisfied customers (74%) play the biggest role in finding new assignments. — [Financieel-Management (KvK report PDF)](https://static.financieel-management.nl/documents/15374/zzp'ers-en-hun-opdrachten.pdf)
- [VERIFIED FACT - survey, date not captured; source attribution uncertain] ZZP Barometer with Yacht (n=1,224): 59.4% get assignments by word of mouth; 41.6% mainly via an intermediary or marketplace; 38.3% via physical networking. — [MijnZZP](https://www.mijnzzp.nl/Weblogs/Persberichten/Referenties-zzp-ers-en-opdrachtgevers-belangrijk-bij-keuze-voor-intermediair)
- [VERIFIED FACT] CBS/TNO: about 6% of ZZP'ers acquire assignments or clients (also) via digital platforms; 30% of those depend on platforms for 60-100% of turnover. — [CBS Monitor online platformen 2022](https://www.cbs.nl/nl-nl/longread/rapportages/2023/monitor-online-platformen-2022/4-werkgelegenheid)
- [VERIFIED FACT - 2017 data] ZEA 2017: only 5.2% of ZZP'ers filing profit tax returns acquire work via a platform; platform-ZZP'ers use social media twice as often and intermediaries four times as often as other ZZP'ers. — [ESB](https://esb.nu/een-eerste-verkenning-van-platform-zzpers/)
- [UNVERIFIED - source not identified in results] One survey reported 23.3% of ZZP'ers actively use marketplaces to generate assignments. — likely [ZZP Nederland kennisbank](https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/kennisbank/zzp-opdrachten-vinden-vergelijk-platforms-en-marktplaatsen)

### Inferences
- After 15+ years of Werkspot and a decade of gig apps, platforms have not displaced the network; the "go-to marketplace" position is still vacant for most ZZP'ers. This can be read two ways: unmet need, or evidence that trust-based work resists marketplace intermediation.
- A product that amplifies word of mouth (portable references, referral mechanics among past clients) aligns with how work is actually won, rather than competing with it via anonymous leads.

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 Knab or ZiPconomy survey on acquisition channels retrieved; no tradesman/construction-specific channel split.
- Percentages come from different years, definitions and samples and are not comparable.
- ZEA 2023/2025 (TNO/CBS) likely contain updated platform-use figures but were not opened: [ZEA in Vogelvlucht 2025](https://monitorarbeid.tno.nl/wp-content/uploads/sites/16/2025/07/ZEA-in-Vogelvlucht-2025.pdf).
